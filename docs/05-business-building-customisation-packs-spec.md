Owns the optional pack model, fourteen specialisation dimensions, activation, precedence, core effects, metrics, evaluation content, distribution contract and complete authoring process. Specification 06 owns the actual curated profiles and concrete showcase inputs; specification 04 owns assessment and run evidence.

“Must” states a requirement; “may” permits a bounded option. Examples and authored controls remain synthetic. A specification, complete record or passing calculation does not establish customer validation, installed behaviour, professional clearance or permission for an external action. Earlier research dates, source IDs and stage references record provenance; the ownership table in specification 01 identifies the current normative document. Where an incorporated record names an earlier stage, implement its completed contract in this specification set, not a missing future design.

## 1. Pack contract and qualification

A Business Extension Pack is a self-contained optional set of instructions/resources for a recurring business mechanism. It specialises existing command questions, required evidence, artefact detail, metrics, experiment choices and checks. It supplies no local customer facts, action approval or replacement core. Core must remain useful and responsible without it. A correct tie is allowed; a title, longer answer or manufactured failure of core is not meaningful benefit.

| Record concern | Required content |
|---|---|
| Identity and scope | Stable business-mechanism name, version, owner, supported core/command contract versions, purpose, maturity evidence and source/review date. A title is not compatibility evidence. |
| Applicability | Positive mechanism facts, required selection or confirmation, excluded/non-activation cases, unknown-fact behaviour and exact component/decision scope. Separate applicability from permission to activate. |
| Catalogue justification | Compared core and existing candidates, inadequate behaviour, overlap and why a smaller revision or scoped mode is insufficient. Keep the rejected alternatives and their evidence. |
| Core effects | For each effect: trigger, affected canonical command, required inputs, changed question/output/check, inherited invariants, evidence basis, and an observable positive/negative comparison. |
| Business and metric semantics | Relevant dimensions below; definitions, units, cohort/window, formulas, cost identities, cash timing, assumptions and source limitations. Do not invent default business performance. |
| Constraints and precedence | Applicable professional findings, facts and accepted decisions; forbidden overrides, conflicts and review triggers. No stored credential or fabricated consent record. |
| Showcase and evaluation | Exact prompt, fixed inputs and versions, core and pack conditions, criteria, actual outputs/receipts where run, negative/non-activation/precedence cases and repair record. |
| Distribution and maintenance | Required local references/assets/scripts, dependencies and licences, reproducibility entry points, compatibility checks, retirement/review triggers and accurate catalogue status. |

## 2. Permitted business-model dimensions

| ID | Original dimension | Legitimate effect and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| D01 | customer / buyer structure | Identify mechanism-relevant users, payers, approvers, suppliers, contributors or beneficiaries in B02/G04. Do not manufacture personas or assume these roles coincide. |
| D02 | offer grammar | Add scoped entitlements, deliverables, acceptance, ongoing work or dependencies in B03. Preserve substantiation, rights and explicit exclusions. |
| D03 | pricing unit | Compare seats, metered use, engagements, visits, licences or intermediary fees in B04. A unit is a choice supported by evidence, not a compulsory price or premium multiplier. |
| D04 | money model | Reconcile initial, recurring and complementary exchanges in B05. Separate own consideration, pass-through funds and funding; no duplicated revenue. |
| D05 | channel priorities | Add mechanism-specific fit questions in G01/G02/G03. Do not mandate SEO, ads, outreach or a provider without audience and downstream evidence. |
| D06 | sales path | Add qualification, approval, purchase and acceptance requirements in G04/G05. Seller activity, an account or a listing is not a buying decision. |
| D07 | delivery model | Add actual work, entitlement, inventory, matching or content-access dependencies in B06. Operators still own fulfilment and production. |
| D08 | retention model | Distinguish renewal, repeat use, matched-side participation, continuing support and content access in G06/G07. Continued billing alone never proves value. |
| D09 | unit economics | Add context-specific cost and cohort bridges in B07/E03. Reuse N, K and C and HC01–HC08; no unexplained LTV/CAC or modelled certainty. |
| D10 | capacity constraints | Add shared human, service, geographic, inventory or maintenance constraints in B06/G08. Do not create a scheduler, invent staff or treat nominal hours as a schedule guarantee. |
| D11 | cash behaviour | Add actual collection, settlement, stock, refund and remaining-obligation dates in B07/E03. Profit and ending cash cannot override an earlier cash gap. |
| D12 | core metrics | Define appropriate numerator, denominator, unit, window and missingness in E01–E04. A new label or source dashboard is not a universal business score. |
| D13 | experiment types | Add adequate mechanism-specific tests in B09/E04, using the cheapest valid evidence. Freeze outcomes and guardrails; a prompt or hypothetical preference is not an executed test. |
| D14 | quality criteria | Add acceptance, access, supply quality, version integrity or client-transfer checks in evaluations. Safeguards and truthfulness must not be moved into an optional pack or averaged away. |

## 3. Qualification and rejection rules

A candidate qualifies as a designed grammar only when all of the following are supported: a recurring mechanism distinct from a label; a bounded insufficiency in core/catalogue handling; explicit reusable effects on named commands; coherent economic and operational consequences; fit and non-fit conditions; preserved invariants and precedence; and a discriminating showcase/evaluation design. A pack declared implemented or validated must additionally supply the actual execution and packaging evidence required for that claim.

No minimum number of changed dimensions or sources is imposed. One important, reusable distinction can justify specialisation; many superficial changes cannot. The catalogue's eight independent candidates are a result of comparison, not a quota. A research candidate may be retained while its promised improvement remains unmeasured. Do not call it a validated installed pack.

| Label-only proposal | Required disposition | A different legitimate mechanism that could qualify |
|---|---|---|
| AI startup | Reject the label alone. | A metered hosted service may use SaaS; variable inference costs are explicit inputs, not a reason to invent an AI executive team. |
| finance company | Reject the industry label alone; route regulated questions separately. | A consumer subscription or professional-service mechanism may qualify, with the actual specialist constraints remaining above pack defaults. |
| fitness business | Reject the industry label alone. | Stock sales, scheduled local coaching and digital content have different mechanisms and may use different scoped grammars. |
| London business | Reject the location label alone. | Travel-constrained appointments can use local-service; a remote London consultant need not. |

A provider bundle, branded book doctrine, mandatory tactic, universal score, copied restricted skill, autonomous outbound engine or duplicated core does not become legitimate by being called a pack. Licensing and business suitability are separate checks. A useful method can be referenced or independently adapted without importing its unsupported numeric claims.

## 4. Activation and precedence

First inspect the actual requested task and supplied project facts. Identify a fitting candidate and explain its scope. Apply a pack only under an explicit selection or an already approved selection policy; otherwise propose it and continue the adequate core path. Unknown mechanism facts produce a question or a bounded core result, not invented fit. An explicitly requested but incompatible pack is not applied; preserve the request and explain the specific mismatch. Failure to activate a pack does not erase the user's task or justify unrelated redesign.

Resolve each proposed default against these levels, in this order:

1. **Verified legal/regulatory constraints and explicit project facts/instructions.** A legal conclusion must be attributable, current and applicable to the actual facts. Facts describe what is true, not what is permitted. Conflicting instructions, facts or applicable findings require bounded clarification or specialist resolution; never pretend one changes the other. No user instruction or pack waives the core's truthfulness boundary.
2. **Approved business decisions.** Preserve their accepted versions and obligations unless an authorised, evidence-grounded revision changes the implicated part. A newly supplied conflicting fact triggers review, not silent overwriting or denial of the fact.
3. **Selected pack defaults.** Apply only within the approved mechanism and component scope, at the identified version. Defaults remain defaults rather than observations.
4. **Core defaults.** Fill only genuinely open choices. Core mandatory evidence, rights, mutation and independent growth constraints remain invariants, not low-priority defaults that a pack can override.

A pack proposing a £100 monthly option cannot overwrite an accepted £80 price. A project instruction to keep a one-time licence cannot be converted into renewal because the pack prefers subscriptions. A known permission violation is rejected; a missing applicable finding blocks the affected commitment. A calculation or design pass cannot substitute for action authority.

Store the selected pack/version, affected command and scope alongside the ordinary decision record. List applied defaults, skipped defaults and conflicts with their reasons. Do not create a second authoritative dossier. Inputs retain source dates and synthetic labels; output quality and permissions are evaluated independently of activation success.

## 5. Composition, evolution and withdrawal

Use at most one primary grammar for a bounded exchange by default. For a hybrid business, define explicit component scopes and a reconciliation record before composing more: who pays for each exchange, which inputs and costs are shared, which obligations interact, and which precedence resolves overlapping defaults. An unresolved overlap blocks that combined default, not a made-up last-loaded-wins choice. The economics of shared work and money are counted once.

Consulting is an advisory mode inside professional-services, not a required inherited parent package. Mobile and SaaS may share underlying concepts without one importing a sibling installation. A marketplace selling its own stock needs separately labelled intermediary and owned-inventory flows. A digital product with coaching separates content access from limited human work. These designs need ordinary scoped references, not a universal graph or pack execution engine.

A pack update does not automatically revise accepted business decisions. Record changed effects, compatibility and affected evidence; rerun the applicable comparisons before acceptance. Historical outputs retain their original pack/core versions. Withdrawal stops applying future defaults but preserves existing customer rights, obligations, evidence and valid decisions. An invalid pack effect is repaired at the pack layer; change core only when evidence demonstrates a core defect, then rerun affected pack checks.

Retire or merge a pack when adequate core behaviour or another candidate meets its complete scope with less duplication. Reopen deferred abstractions only with repeated relevant production evidence under the family extraction rule. No registry promotion, publication, installation or live business effect follows automatically from this design.

## 6. Concrete core-effect and metric records

Every effect has a stable ID, trigger and bounded component; affected canonical commands/modes; required factual inputs; changed question, output or check; inherited invariants; source/uncertainty; positive witness; negative/incompatible witness; and paired evaluation criterion. Its input/metric definitions reuse specification 02, including HC01–HC08 and GG01–GG07. Effect IDs remain PK01-E1 through PK09-E3; PK04 identifies advisory-mode effects inside professional-services, not another package.

Do not store universal prices, conversion rates, LTV, churn, platform fees or legal conclusions as facts. A configurable scenario value has provenance and explicit status. Metrics state formula/event, numerator, denominator, population/cohort, unit, period, revenue/cost basis, actual versus forecast, source, method and limits. Missing values, zero denominators and inapplicability remain distinct. Account/usage, paid entitlement, service effort, stock/funds, content rights and maintenance metrics must reconcile to the same core money and resource identities.

## 7. Packaging

The project chooses an explicit directory convention; it is not an upstream host pack protocol. An implemented pack directory is `extension-packs/<name>/` with the following local contents:

| Local file or directory | Required contents |
|---|---|
| `PACK.md` | Identity/version/owner, compatible core contract and tested revisions, mechanism fit/non-fit, explicit activation, scope, precedence, effects index, source/maturity limits and direct resource menu |
| `references/effects.md` | Every full effect record and metric definition, unchanged safeguards and composition/conflict rules |
| `references/sources.md` | Actually inspected sources, dates/limits, independent synthesis and source/licence/notice boundaries |
| `showcase/prompt.txt` and `showcase/input.json` | Literal complete fixed task and all declared input facts; explicit synthetic labels and protected versions; no unresolved placeholders |
| `evals/criteria.md` and fixed case inputs | All seven evaluation obligations, positive/negative/incompatible/unknown/precedence/preservation controls and expected findings kept outside the subject input |
| `evidence/` | Actual run/assessment records needed for the claimed state, with versions, both paired arms, errors, repairs and limitations; no fabricated pass file |
| `manifest.json` | Relative path/hash, required/optional role, core/pack contract identity and actual prerequisites; no secrets or implicit sibling dependency |

Use focused additional local resources only when actually needed. A PACK.md index may point to sources for optional provenance, but all mandatory operating instructions and referenced case inputs ship locally. Source/licence checks apply to the actual contents; a root licence never silently grants rights in outside material. No runtime resolver or installer is required by the convention.

The consumer explicitly provides/selects the pack directory or uses a tested installation binding. The core skill reads its menu, checks fit and compatible contract, applies only selected effects and records skipped/conflicting defaults. Do not depend on automatic host discovery. Unknown compatibility is unresolved, not inferred from similar names. A missing required file blocks that effect; an adequate core task can continue with its factual limits.

An advisory mode is bundled with professional-services and selected explicitly when it fits. Other packs contain their own required content, with no parent/sibling import. For multiple scoped exchanges, reconcile shared money, human work and obligations once before composition. Updates, removal or retirement preserve historical outputs and customer rights and cannot silently revise accepted business decisions.

## 8. Authoring workflow

### 1. Authoring unit and authority

The unit is one bounded new or revised business-model grammar at an identified core version. A new business, new industry, new provider or interesting book is not automatically a new pack. Start with the actual catalogue and the requested mechanism. An empty installed catalogue can be a verified fact without establishing that every possible external capability is absent.

Use the existing P01–P07 commands; the eleven steps below refine their workflow without creating eleven extra skills. Each step preserves source facts, versions, unresolved issues and its permitted output. An author may draft, calculate and run authorised local checks. A plan does not authorise live communications, billing, ad spend, customer-data migration or professional conclusions. No pack changes its own acceptance criteria while presenting the result as a comparable rerun.

### 2. Complete eleven-step workflow

| ID | Original step | Owning command and inputs | Required activity and durable output | Exit, failure and smallest repair |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A01 | inspect catalogue | P01; actual catalogue, core version, requested mechanism and project constraints. | Read relevant entries and contents, implementation/maturity evidence and compatibility. Record scope, evidence limits and suitable existing options, including a verified empty catalogue where applicable. | Unknown catalogue contents are not proof of insufficiency. Recover the relevant record or state the exact missing input; no automatic new pack or installation. |
| A02 | prove existing pack is insufficient | P01; the inspection and a concrete task/failure case. | Compare core only, existing pack, bounded mode/revision and new candidate. Identify the exact behaviour not adequately served and why a smaller option fails. Retain the comparison and proposed disposition. | A name, convenience or untested absence claim fails. Revise the scope, select the adequate existing option or stop new-pack authoring when no residual exists. |
| A03 | research business-model practice | P02; bounded residual, mechanism and required decisions. | Inspect appropriate primary practice/empirical sources, definitions, contradictions, legal handoffs and date/read limits. Link each proposed distinction to evidence or an explicit testable hypothesis. | Do not fill missing evidence with universal price/channel advice. Repair retrieval or qualify the claim; an unresolved mandatory input blocks the affected authoring decision. |
| A04 | define changed core behaviour | P03; researched mechanism, core contracts and catalogue comparison. | Write complete effects with trigger, canonical command, input, changed output/check, inherited invariant and test. Define activation/non-activation and explicit component scope. | A no-effect label, hidden core replacement or incompatible mutation right fails. Repair the specific effect or merge with the adequate candidate. |
| A05 | define economics and constraints | P03 with B07/HC01–HC08 calculation handoff; actual definitions and proposed effect. | Document payer/unit, cost/consideration and cash bridges, capacity, retention dependencies, metrics and specialist boundaries. Reuse deterministic tools for calculations and retain uncertainty. | Arithmetic success does not validate assumptions. Reconcile duplicate costs, incompatible units or missing dates before relying on the model; do not implement an accounting engine. |
| A06 | build showcase | P04; exact versions, task, fixed evidence and permitted local scope. | Create a complete bounded scenario that visibly exercises the effects and inherited safeguards. Supply facts, sources, assumptions, configuration, expected distinctions and actual artefacts/receipts when executed. | A marketing description or invented success is not a showcase. Repair missing artefacts or execute the required run; preserve failed outcomes rather than fabricate replacements. |
| A07 | include exact prompt | P04; the complete showcase inputs and task. | Save the literal copyable prompt, including scope, input locators, versions, selected condition and expected deliverable. Resolve placeholders before calling an example reproducible. Keep the core and pack task identical except for declared condition selection. | Missing inputs, hidden conversation dependencies or different tasks invalidate a paired claim. Repair the prompt/input record and repeat affected runs. |
| A08 | create positive / negative evals | P05; effects, invariants, showcase and likely misuse. | Define activation, non-activation, precedence, changed behaviour, pack metrics, incompatible/negative and preservation cases. Freeze dimension-specific pass/fail/block criteria before interpreting results. Keep exact prompts and expected failures. | All-positive or non-discriminating cases fail. Add the smallest counterexample without weakening existing criteria; separate deterministic checks from business judgement. |
| A09 | compare core vs core+pack | P06; same fixed task/data, exact core/pack versions and frozen criteria. | Actually run both required conditions with compatible settings and retain outputs, method, failures and deviations. Compare specialised coverage, correct metrics, preservation and resource measures only where actually observed. | Expectations or one completed arm are insufficient for an executed comparison. Missing paired evidence is BLOCKED; broken invariants FAIL. An inconclusive contrast remains inconclusive, not superior. |
| A10 | validate | P07; definition, all required showcase/evaluation/compatibility evidence and unchanged tested version. | Review substance and execute applicable structural, calculation, behaviour and packaging checks. Record separate findings, source/licence limits, actual receipts, repairs and the exact scope of any accepted claim. | Repair the owning defect and rerun affected checks. No validated implemented-pack status from a design-only transcript, stale result, missing run or convenient aggregate score. |
| A11 | catalogue | P07; passing evidence for the claimed state and explicit catalogue-edit authority. | Add/revise the exact local entry with version, fit, effects, compatibility, sources, prompt, evaluation evidence and honest maturity. Preserve history and superseded versions. | A research catalogue may list a clearly unimplemented candidate; it may not advertise it as validated. Missing publication/registry authority blocks those separate actions, not creation of an authorised validation report. |

The order is a dependency sequence, not a requirement to repeat every unchanged research activity on a typo correction. A material change to applicability, economics, precedence or core effects reopens the affected steps and comparisons. Preserve evidence supporting unaffected behaviour. All required acceptance for the **new claim/version** must still exist before that version receives the claim.

### 3. Showcase and paired-comparison record

A reproducible record contains the exact task and fixed input identities; synthetic/observed/source labels; accepted decisions and rights; core and pack versions; selected condition and activation basis; tool/host and relevant settings; permitted actions; frozen criteria; actual outputs and failures; evaluator method; limitations; and decision/repair. Reuse ordinary documents and files. Sensitive source data stays in authorised custody; public examples use synthetic or specifically approved/redacted material.

For a paired comparison, both conditions receive the same project facts, task, calculation tools and core safeguards. The pack condition adds only the declared specialisation. Record unavoidable environmental or stochastic differences. Where repetitions are necessary to support a reliability claim, justify them and retain all runs; no arbitrary universal sample quota is introduced. No token, latency, cost or efficacy number is reported without an actual compatible measurement.

Core is not required to fail. It may discover the same mechanism-specific issue. A legitimate pack might make coverage more systematic or produce a useful business-specific artefact. If repeated adequate core behaviour makes the pack redundant, merge or retire it. Do not create a false positive advantage by leaving core with fewer facts or disabled safeguards. If the pack changes an answer because it adds an unsupported assumption, that is not improvement.

#### Exact authoring request template

The following is a copyable template, not a completed primary example. Replace the angle-bracket fields with accessible concrete values before a claimed reproducible run.

```text
Use business-pack-author to assess <mechanism> against core <version> and catalogue <path/version>. Read <fixed input paths> and preserve <accepted decision versions and rights>. Complete catalogue inspection and insufficiency analysis before proposing a new pack. Research the mechanism, define named core effects, economics, constraints, activation and non-activation. Prefer an adequate existing pack or bounded revision. Produce the scoped definition and evidence, not an installation, release or live business action. Ask only for a genuinely missing required decision or input.
```

#### Exact showcase/comparison request template

```text
Evaluate the bounded decision in <fixture path and identity> using core <version>, condition <core-only or core-plus-pack: pack/version>. Use the same task, inputs, calculations and frozen criteria at <criteria path> for both conditions. Preserve <accepted versions and rights>. Return the business assessment, explicit assumptions, actual calculations, applicable separate gates and smallest justified next step. Do not send, spend, charge, publish, change source decisions or claim missing observations. Save actual outputs and run details. Compare both completed conditions by criterion; label synthetic data and all unmeasured claims.
```

#### Exact negative/precedence request template

```text
Use <selected pack/version> on <fixed fixture and identity>. The fixture contains a proposed default that conflicts with <explicit fact, approved decision, right or applicable constraint>. Assess that proposal without editing the original. Identify the conflict, preserve the higher-priority item and return a bounded repair or unresolved question. A favourable conversion or contribution result must not override the conflict. Record whether the pack is applicable separately from whether the proposed action is permitted.
```

The design-probe companion supplies concrete fixed prompts and data without these placeholders. Templates here define authoring responsibilities; their existence does not establish that later installed showcase runs have happened.

### 4. Evaluation dimensions and acceptance

| Dimension | Positive evidence | Failure or unresolved state |
|---|---|---|
| Activation | The selected pack fits the actual mechanism and named scope; required context and compatible versions are present. | Industry-name inference, unapproved selection, incompatible core or unknown material fit does not activate automatically. |
| Non-activation | Core can complete a suitable bounded task without the pack; the non-fit case retains its original workflow. | Mandatory pack dependency, false trigger or silent additional defaults fails the contract. |
| Precedence | Explicit facts, applicable constraints and approved decisions remain intact; skipped defaults have reasons. | Pack overrides, hidden source edits or averaging away an independent gate fail. Unresolved higher-level conflicts remain blocked. |
| Changed business behaviour | The output exercises the named effect on an actual command: relevant questions, artefact fields, checks or decision detail. | More prose, a new title or a generic checklist alone is no meaningful effect. |
| Pack-specific metrics | Relevant units, denominators, windows and economic/cash bases are explicit and calculations reconcile. | Inverted fractions, duplicate funds/costs, obsolete provider defaults or unsupported LTV cannot pass. |
| Negative / incompatible cases | Harmful proposals are rejected and legitimate controls retained; incomplete inputs remain unknown. | Blanket refusal of legitimate mechanisms is not a successful safety test. Missing evidence is not a zero or favourable result. |
| Core vs core+pack difference | Actual compatible paired outputs are retained and judged separately, including equality and shortcomings. | Written expectations, an unrun arm, changed task or cherry-picked result cannot establish measured difference. |
| Preservation and repair | Change only the evidenced responsible layer, retain valid earlier decisions and rerun affected checks. | Broad rewrites, erased failed evidence or weakened criteria invalidate acceptance. |

For design acceptance, require complete content, relevant research, coherent effects, exact prompts/cases and appropriate executed design checks. For implemented/installed acceptance, also require the actual pack resources, required paired host execution and clean installation evidence at matching versions. These are different claims, not interchangeable maturity labels. A passing Stage 14 does not pre-approve any of the eight candidate implementations.

## 9. Acceptance and maintenance

All eight content responsibilities, relevant dimensions, explicit effects and compatible local files must be complete for a design/distribution claim. Implemented/validated claims additionally require actual installed showcases, both paired conditions, all seven evaluation obligations, create and revise authoring paths, preservation and clean installation at the same tested identities. The evaluator cannot edit the subject pack or weaken a criterion to obtain a pass. State every failure and missing mandatory result.

Catalogue research can legitimately retain a NOT IMPLEMENTED candidate; it cannot advertise installed availability. Recheck material mechanism/source/core changes, preserve old evidence and rerun affected cases. Repeated adequate core coverage can justify shrinking, merging or retiring a pack; record the demonstrated reason. No new universal ontology, commercial score, experiment engine or automatic publication follows from the authoring workflow.
