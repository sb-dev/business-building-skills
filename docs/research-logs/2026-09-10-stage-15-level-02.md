A coherent draft offer/price/acceptance record, capacity and cash calculation, risk/proof handoff and bounded test. Do not create unrelated outputs or replace source evidence with the assessment. A blocked commitment can still have a complete, useful design or review record.

### Discriminating acceptance

### Negative control

Fail a low-price win that conceals negative contribution, unlimited capacity, a certain client benefit, or treating the day-30 balance as day-14 funding.

### Preservation and smallest responsible repair

Preserve the customer/problem baseline. Revise only proposed commercial scope or timing with its owner; do not create a new business model to avoid the cash calculation.
