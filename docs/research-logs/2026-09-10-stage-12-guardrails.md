## 1. All thirteen original exclusions

Retain every named concern in the original order. The generic mechanisms are deferred because no accepted evidence justifies them; deception, unlawful spam and a universal quality score also conflict with explicit governing principles. A later requirement cannot waive those principles merely by claiming demand. Reopening requires a bounded legitimate need, inadequate simpler alternatives, repeated relevant evidence and the necessary authority. It does not mean automatically implementing the deferred idea.

| ID | Original idea | Current disposition and reason | Adequate smaller approach / evidence required to reopen |
|---|---|---|---|
| D01 | universal business ontology | Defer. Stage 4’s small dossier and named evidence/decision links meet the identified needs. | Use the minimum necessary fields. Reopen only for repeated incompatible cases that cannot be represented without a justified shared concept; no global schema for its own sake. |
| D02 | universal CRM | Defer; operational state is explicitly external under Stage 11, and the assessed CRM already supplies records and authorised mutations. | Use a suitable incumbent and authorised exports. A missing integration can justify one adapter, not rebuilding contact/pipeline ownership. |
| D03 | custom ad platform | Defer; Stage 10 already identifies platform execution, while the residual concerns commercial judgement rather than bidding or campaign infrastructure. | Use authorised operator/UI/API boundaries. An evidenced unsupported operation requires specific review, not a general bidding system. |
| D04 | custom payment system | Defer; processing and financial state are external, and no evidenced business-judgement gap requires a new transaction or settlement implementation. | Use a suitable provider and exact approved operation. Missing business reasoning does not justify handling funds or reinventing settlement. |
| D05 | custom analytics platform | Defer; measurement and analysis capabilities already exist, while inconsistent definitions require a semantic bridge rather than another event platform. | Reconcile definitions and use adequate queries/exports. Reopen only an evidenced narrow missing capability, not a replacement warehouse. |
| D06 | custom accounting engine | Defer; accounting records and treatment belong to systems and qualified people, and managerial contribution analysis does not transfer those responsibilities. | Reuse reviewed records and deterministic calculations. A contribution formula is not a ledger requirement or accounting certification. |
| D07 | universal growth graph | Defer; accepted records and links already preserve the business path, and no repeated production case establishes the need for a graph runtime. | Use simple linked claims and decisions. Require repeated production evidence of an unsolved cross-record need before any shared graph proposal. |
| D08 | autonomous outbound-spam engine | Reject the spam objective under §§5,35. No commercial benefit can justify deceptive or unlawful outreach. | An explicitly authorised legitimate communication task uses existing sending/suppression controls. That is a different bounded responsibility, not a reopening of spam. |
| D09 | persistent cross-project customer database | Defer; unnecessary duplication expands data-purpose exposure without resolving an evidenced business decision that cannot use authorised source records. | Keep evidence in authorised project/source custody and use controlled locators. Any cross-project use needs a specific lawful purpose and permission, not convenience. |
| D10 | one universal business score | Reject as the quality/approval mechanism under §5. It could conceal an independent rights, capacity or cash block. | Keep dimensions and gates separate. A narrowly defined descriptive metric cannot substitute for a whole-business approval score. |
| D11 | universal experiment platform | Defer; assignment, collection and inference have existing owners, and choosing an adequate test does not require replacing their execution machinery. | Use the cheapest adequate test and appropriate infrastructure. Reopen only a demonstrated narrow unsupported function without recreating the entire platform. |
| D12 | universal pricing optimiser | Defer; scope, payer, evidence and constraints vary; the project owns reasoning, not unconstrained price optimisation. | Compare bounded alternatives with explicit assumptions. A specific justified calculation does not establish a universal optimiser. |
| D13 | multi-agent virtual executive team | Defer; responsibility rows do not imply autonomous executive roles or a fixed agent team. | Use the smallest coherent skills and explicit human approvals. Add a specialist role only for demonstrated independent value and verified boundary, not a job-title collection. |

The 13-row list is a scope control, not a proposed roadmap to build thirteen products. The native shortlist cannot own these systems indirectly by renaming them orchestration, intelligence or memory.
