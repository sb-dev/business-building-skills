## 3. Analysis, comparisons and decisions

The six files are operating contracts with deliberately distinct owners. System invariants belong to 01; business meanings and procedures to 02; installation and mutation interfaces to 03; assessment and real-run evidence to 04; reusable specialisation to 05; actual curated profiles/prompts to 06. Source/command/evaluation IDs retain their namespaces, so evidence E01 is not example or command E01. A conflict is repaired at its owner and traced through affected resources/cases rather than silently selecting whichever statement is convenient.

Full accepted domain modules and all command fields were incorporated to preserve the researched depth. Bootstrap-only exit claims were removed, historical future-stage references in the business modules were mapped to current owners, and the legal routing packet explicitly distinguishes preparation from an authorised transmission. These editorial changes do not modify the accepted source files or weaken their business rules. Complete primary prompts and pack inputs are reproduced exactly; stress and additional adversarial full corpora have pinned normative identities rather than being replaced by shorter newly invented cases.

| Alternative | Decision and reason |
|---|---|
| Six brief outlines plus links to unspecified future work | Reject: fails §25 exit. Required meanings, commands, packaging, cases and gates are concretely specified. |
| Rewrite every accepted contract from memory | Reject: unnecessary loss of evidence and semantic drift. Adopt complete source contracts, with explicit ownership and provenance. |
| One mandatory common runtime or sibling skill to hold safeguards | Reject: selective installation must be independently responsible. Ship local copies and verify common identity plus actual dependency closure. |
| A new code generator or universal installer framework | Reject as unnecessary. Canonical authoring, versioned local copies/manifests and scoped checks satisfy the distribution contract. |
| Assume an Agent Skills host automatically discovers business packs | Reject: this is a project convention. Use explicit PACK.md directories and tested, optional bindings with real activation evidence. |
| Generic command contract with unqualified modes | Reject: preserve every command's nine fields and G02/E02/E03 mode-specific obligations, including assessment-only mutation limits. |
| Treat positive arithmetic or authored controls as installed performance | Reject: require actual captured host runs, both compatible pack arms and separate clean consumer evidence at the assigned stages. |
| Shrink catalogue, examples or stress coverage to simplify implementation | Reject: preserve exact accepted candidates, advisory effects, fifteen primaries and three stress structures. |
| Choose a new business price, provider, jurisdiction or source licence | Reject as outside current evidence. Core uses supplied context; actual rights and implementation support claims require their evidence. |

New concrete repository choices are the skill-local command/core-rule/business-record/pack resource layout, checked identical common rules and dependency manifests, explicit pack directory contents, host-neutral actual-install evidence procedure, and scoped CI contracts. They complete Stage 18 technical ownership without creating a production scaffold. Licence selection/authorisation is a Stage 21 distribution input; it is not an invented grant or missing business architecture. Current source scope supplies no reason to reopen accepted Stages 1–15.
