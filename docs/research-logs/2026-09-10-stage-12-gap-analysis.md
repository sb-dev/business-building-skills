## 1. Decision and scope of the classification

Retain a small native business-judgement surface that composes existing evidence, tools and professional findings. Do not rebuild execution systems or package five book-shaped skills. The native shortlist below contains eight responsibilities, not eight skills or an implemented command set. Stage 13 will test how to group them without losing their required behaviour.

Coverage is assessed against the particular responsibility stated in each row, using the accepted source corpus and the inspected Stage 10 candidates. **covered** means the bounded responsibility already has an adequate reusable input or execution owner; **partially covered** means useful components exist but the required end-to-end judgement or contract is not established by those components; **missing** would mean no assessed component supplies the named responsibility at the inspected scope. These labels do not describe global market absence, legal clearance, customer demand or product maturity.

Research-model coverage and installed-product coverage are separate. Stages 2–11 already define the project’s business model and constraints; they do not establish installed skills. Conversely, a generic plugin’s untested integration is not a missing CRM or statistical algorithm. No candidate is marked missing merely because this repository has not implemented it. All current product-level behaviour still needs its later implementation and acceptance evidence.

The source-derived baseline is the [Stage 2 capability taxonomy](2026-09-09-stage-02-reconciliation-and-taxonomy.md), [Stage 10 landscape](2026-09-10-stage-10-capability-landscape.md), [execution candidates](2026-09-10-stage-10-execution-system-candidates.md) and [reuse findings](2026-09-10-stage-10-reuse-decisions-and-boundaries.md). The [Stage 11 decision](2026-09-10-stage-11-execution-layer.md) fixes execution ownership. Source limitations and unperformed installation tests in those records remain limitations here. The gap analysis is a new synthesis of accepted evidence, not a new audit of every vendor or the five complete books.

## 3. Native capability shortlist

Each NC item is a proposed responsibility justified by the named residual, not a new agent role, skill count or platform. It consumes the accepted core models; it does not repeat their research from scratch. Any later command must map here and retain the narrower evidence-backed scope.

| ID | Native responsibility | Evidenced gap | Necessary output and acceptance implication | Explicitly not owned |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NC01 | Evidence-qualified opportunity, customer and value framing | G02 | A bounded decision with customer/role, problem, alternative and value claims traceable to observations or explicit assumptions; distinguish research from local demand. | Search engine, survey collector, customer database or invention of evidence. |
| NC02 | Coherent offer, pricing and money-model reasoning | G03, G04 | An explicit value exchange and price/scope/terms comparison that consumes reproducible cost, cash, capacity and proof evidence; reject contradictory obligations. | Billing, payment processing, accounting treatment or a universal pricing optimiser. |
| NC03 | Qualified acquisition, sales and retained-growth reasoning | G05, G06, G07 | A channel/path/retention or expansion proposal tied to suitable buyer events and downstream economics; preserve informed choice and actual capacity. | Campaign sending, CRM mutation, bidding, content production or attribution infrastructure. |
| NC04 | Delivery, economics and cash commitment review | G07, G08, G09 | Independent, evidence-linked quality, resource, economic, retention and dated-cash findings that can block growth; missing assumptions remain visible. | Numerical engine, ledger, payroll/tax treatment, inventory or scheduling platform. |
| NC05 | Assumption, experiment and learning discipline | G10, G11 | Frozen decision-changing test contract and honest learning record, including negative, invalid and inconclusive outcomes; choose adequate evidence before minimising effort. | Assignment platform, survey execution, inference library or fabricated test results. |
| NC06 | Constraint diagnosis and preservation-aware repair | G12, G13 | Evidence-qualified owning layer, rival explanations, smallest sufficient change and protected versions; permit justified coupled change without routine whole-business rewrites. | Universal optimiser, automatic root-cause oracle, repair execution or governance system. |
| NC07 | Truthfulness and cross-domain constraint handoffs | G14, G15 | A bounded issue/question, source facts, applicable returned constraints and exact permitted next action; ethics and authority are independent gates. | Legal/tax/investment conclusions, professional certification, autonomous authority or a compliance platform. |
| NC08 | Business-model specialisation qualification and authoring discipline | G16 | Evidence that a reusable specialisation changes meaningful core behaviour, respects precedence and can be compared against core; retain catalogue and review requirements. | Industry-tag proliferation, provider-specific replacement core, pack runtime or presumed successful pack benchmark. |

NC07 is cross-cutting behaviour, not a demand for a stand-alone legal skill inside this family. NC04 interprets calculations rather than owning arithmetic. NC08 is necessary for the bootstrap’s specified pack-authoring capability, not a justification for a virtual executive team. Stage 13 must compare coherent groupings rather than mechanically create eight skills from eight rows.

### Admission and retirement rule

Before adding a native responsibility, name its G residual, source evidence and required observable behaviour; compare adequate reuse or a smaller composition; identify what remains external; and provide a discriminating acceptance case. Reject a proposal whose only reason is convenience, a familiar job title, a book chapter, an untested claim of market absence or unavailable customer credentials.

Re-evaluate when an existing component demonstrably meets the full scoped contract. More adequate reuse can shrink or remove the native responsibility. A new provider release or popularity count alone does not establish that equivalence; inspect the actual behaviour, licensing/access, data and failure boundaries. This is a maintenance rule, not a claim that such a replacement has already been tested.

## 5. Exit and handoff

All original gap areas and the additional pack-authoring need are classified, all C01–C22 responsibilities are traceable, and every native item has a bounded evidenced residual. Existing execution remains with its selected owners. The companion tests proposed unnecessary abstractions and preservation failures. No current-stage user-owned choice or mandatory source remains unresolved.

Stage 13 receives NC01–NC08, RU01–RU07, the exact G residuals, exclusion/reopening rules and acceptance implications. It must choose the smallest coherent skills and commands without inventing a new gap to support a preferred split. Stage 12 is not evidence that those skills already exist. Overall completion requires its verified stage commit and receipt.
