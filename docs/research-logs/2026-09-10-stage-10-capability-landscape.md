## 2. Research and selection method

Discovery used repository searches, current repository catalogues, actual skill files and licences, and official provider/API documentation. Searches covered the original skill, research, commercial, measurement and execution categories. Candidates were retained when they addressed a distinct required responsibility or an important reuse alternative: generative guidance, evidence retrieval, preference/response collection, operational state, analytics/experimentation, payments/accounting, calculation/file handling and hosting.

The final pool is the 26 fully assessed entries TC01–TC26. Product-management and finance plugins share a repository but have different responsibilities; their package scope is explicit. Marketing Skills is one collection candidate, not inflated into dozens of unaudited individual skill assessments. The spreadsheet skill is included because its local licence changes the reuse decision. PostHog and GrowthBook are distinct experiment architectures; Sheets, openpyxl and Decimal are different calculation/file responsibilities, not three interchangeable spreadsheet engines. Two messaging systems and two hosting systems provide concrete alternatives without requiring both in a client project.

Outdated skill-directory names and third-party mirrors were not used as current source authority. Uninspected catalogue entries remain discovery leads, not full audits. The Xero developer overview returned a JavaScript shell; the official OpenAPI repository supplied a readable alternative. An incorrect generic Zendesk route was replaced with its current ticketing reference. These were ordinary retrieval repairs, not reasons to stop. Existing proprietary services were assessed for external use, not copied. No recommendation depends on a star threshold, invented adoption metric or an unperformed customer benchmark.

All selected external material was accessed on **10 September 2026**. The source field states the actual reading boundary; maturity and maintenance fields distinguish documented availability from proven local performance. Git blobs identify selected repository texts; a moving main-branch URL is not claimed to be immutable. No full linked PDF, entire codebase, account configuration, paid API request, live campaign, survey, ledger mutation or deployed site was tested. Source dates are distinct from the installed runtime or the newest package release. The small local Decimal checks in conformance are the only capability executions claimed here.

Licence fields distinguish a source/component licence from hosted-service entitlement, customer-data rights and permission to execute. Where only a commercial service was inspected, the assessment does not claim a comprehensive contractual review. Those terms and actual account entitlements must be checked before real integration; their absence does not prevent the present research decision about whether to reuse an external execution responsibility. Stage 9 owns the handoff for material legal uncertainty.

## 3. Reuse decision meanings

**USE** means a suitable existing execution option for the stated role, conditional on the business context, verified access and later technical acceptance. It does not mean installed, purchased, authorised to mutate, or selected as a universal provider. **ADAPT** means the inspected material contains useful reusable judgement but needs explicit changes; it is not copied into this repository during research. **REFERENCE** means useful context or a possible specialist handoff, not a mandatory runtime dependency. **REJECT** means rejected for the specified proposed use, not a universal judgement about every authorised use of that product.

Provider facts are grounded in the source field. Quality assessments, gap statements and reuse decisions are project analysis under the accepted models. A missing demonstration in the inspected material is not proof that an entire vendor product lacks the capability. Existing tools may already perform anomaly detection, statistical validity checks, forecasting and substantial commercial reasoning; this research does not erase that overlap to justify new skills. This is not a claim that no existing tool can supply a more complete solution; later evidence can justify further reuse and a smaller native surface.
