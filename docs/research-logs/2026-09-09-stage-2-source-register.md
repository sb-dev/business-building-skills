## 1. Evidence boundary

This register records the material actually examined for the five-book capability synthesis. It does **not** establish that five complete books were read.

Two File Library searches using the book titles and then shorter author/title queries did not locate matching source books. Unrelated project documents were excluded. The working evidence is public author/publisher material: selected authorised excerpts, companion checklists, methodology articles and book/course descriptions. Complete books, embedded video lessons and audiobooks were not accessed or transcribed.

The three Offers PDFs were inspected as text and page images, including their diagrams. No source PDFs, screenshots, substantial passages, proprietary examples or chapter structures are redistributed in this repository.

### Source classes

| Code | Material inspected | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| E | Authorised book excerpt | Content of that excerpt, not uninspected chapters |
| C | Author/organisation companion material or authored explanation | The concept in that material; correspondence to a particular book passage remains unverified |
| D | Author/publisher book or course description | Stated scope and high-level claims, not detailed method or demonstrated effectiveness |
| B | Bibliographic/product record | Identity or format metadata only |

These classes describe **access**, not empirical reliability. An authentic author assertion can still be an unsupported generalisation. Repetition across an author's books, course and marketing pages is not independent corroboration. Review count, sales rank and promotional results are not efficacy evidence.

## 5. Open source-coverage gaps

| Gap | Consequence | Closure condition |
|---|---|---|
| G-O: complete Offers methods and edition not inspected | Companion-derived candidates remain labelled; no complete book extraction claim | Examine locatable authorised passages for any source-specific rule proposed for implementation |
| G-L: Leads playbooks and exceptions not inspected | No inferred scripts, targeting playbooks or performance promises from promotional descriptions | Inspect the relevant source methods, definitions and caveats before adopting those specifics |
| G-M: Money Models mechanics and quantitative definitions not inspected | No mandatory offer ladder or financial formula inferred from a synopsis | Verify relevant method text and independently test calculation assumptions |
| G-P: selected Personal MBA excerpts only | Do not infer that unexamined topics are absent from the book | Expand direct excerpts where needed and record edition correspondence when available |
| G-R: complete Lean Startup edition not inspected | Author methodology can inform candidates; edition-specific attribution remains open | Verify the relevant book passages before attributing precise methods to that edition |
| G-E: no independent empirical verification in Stage 2 | Authentic sourcing is not efficacy evidence | Stage 3 evaluates professional practice, independent evidence and failure conditions |

These gaps permit a provisional capability synthesis and independent Stage 3 research. They prevent claiming exhaustive five-book coverage or treating uninspected methods as settled rules. A lawful, locatable excerpt can close a specific attribution gap without republishing the source.

When a gap closes, update affected concept rows, conflicts and source records together. Record whether new evidence confirms, qualifies or contradicts the previous interpretation.
