## 1. Purpose and isolation

High-ticket B2B professional services constrained by one person, client dependencies and long collections.

Execute each design as an independent local review when an appropriate installed product is available. Stage 16 does not claim such an execution. The exact accepted base prompt is copied below without alteration. For a base run, use that prompt followed by the canonical stress instruction and coverage requirements. For an adversarial run, append exactly one numbered overlay and its review request. An overlay is a new synthetic review subject, never a change to the accepted source; reset to the base before the next overlay. Additional observations, proposals, and counterfactuals retain their stated scope. In particular, separate incident windows must not be merged into the baseline financial totals without identity evidence.

The failing and adequate candidates are human-authored design controls. They are not captured agent outputs, executed benchmark scores, customer research or professional findings. Keep them outside the future agent's input. The adequate candidate resolves the stated defect only; it does not imply every growth gate passes. A correct answer may reject a proposal or leave an affected commitment blocked. Do not demand an optimistic commercial decision as a condition of passing the reasoning review.

Use the existing selectors with explicit modes: evaluate-component takes one of opportunity, offer, pricing, money-model, channel, funnel or retention for its identified subject; evaluate-unit-economics uses scope=both when the review covers economics and cash; design-acquisition declares its outreach or content-loop mode. Separate assessments when subjects differ. scale-channel means an assessment/proposal under the existing growth gates, not permission to execute.

Expected output is one coherent dossier with the fifteen accepted business-map concerns, evidence/assumption/metric/interpretation/decision separation, a comparison of alternatives, reproducible economics and dated cash, scoped claims/handoffs, a diagnosis, an RR01–RR08 repair proposal where implicated, and an EX01–EX09 evidence programme with LR01–LR08 learning record. These can be sections, not compulsory separate files. Every future reviewed output must identify actual input/prompt/core/pack versions, calculations performed, limits and protected versions. Never relabel the supplied synthetic observations as live commercial evidence.

Apply GG01 evidence/definitions, GG02 value/quality, GG03 capacity, GG04 economics, GG05 retained/downstream value, GG06 dated usable cash and GG07 rights/professional constraints/authority separately. A failure or missing relevant input cannot be averaged away. Report PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED or justified NOT APPLICABLE for each bounded gate. This is the inherited business decision model; an expected FAIL on a deliberately bad proposal is successful defect detection, not a failed Stage 16 design.

Each proposed specialist handoff is a draft packet using Stage 9 HF01–HF07: exact issue/action, preserved facts and versions, business intention, assumptions, bounded question/owner, required returned constraint, and affected process review. No message is sent. Current external legal, platform, market or cost facts must be verified before a real commitment; no such new claim is supplied by this synthetic design.

Use core without any pack as the baseline. An explicitly selected fitting pack may apply only the accepted named effects. It must retain all project facts, approved decisions and mandatory invariants; identical correct outcomes are valid. An unselected or incompatible pack does not activate. The architecture comparison here is a design trace, not a measured core-versus-pack performance result.

**Failing candidate (authored negative control):** Book all twelve calls because no second project has been signed yet.

**Required discriminating findings:**

- The baseline one-project workload is 114 hours, leaving six nominal hours. Twelve additional calls at 1.5 hours add 18, reaching 132, twelve over capacity before any new sale.

- Lead fit, permission, existing work and actual time distribution remain relevant; extra volume cannot displace accepted delivery without approval.

**Adequate candidate (authored comparison control):** Reject the all-calls commitment. Propose triage and a bounded schedule within verified spare time; keep surplus prospects uncommitted and do not contact them from this exercise.

## 7. Bounded evidence programme

The following is a complete next-evidence design. No recruitment, customer test or external action has occurred. Numeric recruitment or inference requirements belong to a later authorised study if its decision requires them; the finite record audits and qualitative diagnostics below make no statistical claim.

| Stage 8 experiment field | Case-specific design |
|---|---|
| assumption | The accepted bounded project can be delivered within its full allowed work envelope without delaying existing obligations; nominal monthly hours alone do not prove this. |
| hypothesis | A task/dependency replay of the stipulated one-project engagement fits the real available calendar, includes follow-up and non-delivery work, and exposes any missing client access/acceptance prerequisites. |
| cheapest valid test | First audit the existing task, calendar and collection records. This is cheaper and more relevant than buying leads or promising a second project. If a missing delivery estimate remains, propose a separately authorised bounded diagnostic, not a full transformation. |
| target population | The single defined engagement, owner resource and client dependencies. Generalisation to another buyer or system is explicitly untested. |
| success failure signal | Reconciled workload at or below 120 hours is necessary but insufficient; due-date fit, quality and client inputs also need evidence. Missing time or known overlap challenges readiness. Cash below 500 independently blocks the proposed commitment. |
| guardrails | No contact, client-data access, implementation, contract change, hiring or new promise here. Any later replay using private records requires access authority; actual delivery needs approvals and funding. Preserve bounded scope and one-person capacity. |
| duration sample | Inspect all tasks and commitments in the delivery month and collections through day 60, extending the calendar if payment slips. This is a complete scoped reconciliation, not a statistical sample or evidence of repeatable market demand. |
| confounders | Unrecorded rework, omitted selling/admin time, deferred work, client delays, overlapping resource assignments and conflating contract signature with collection. |
| decision rule | Known overload or cash gap: propose containment at intake/funding layer, not new sales. Adequate dated fit: consider only one bounded next commitment subject to remaining gates and owner approval. Missing records: block feasibility conclusion. Inconclusive estimates: request a bounded measurement. Guardrail breach: stop affected work through its owner and retain evidence. |

The learning record must preserve the hypothesis, this unexecuted design, supplied fictional observations, actual deterministic results, qualified interpretation, bounded decision, remaining unknowns and proposed next commitment. Do not backfill a prespecified rule into the inherited observations. Reconsider only affected dependencies after new evidence; retain source and accepted versions.
