## 2. Actual comparisons and selected reuse decisions

These decisions apply to the proposed use in this repository, not to all possible uses of a vendor product. No price ranking or paid-plan selection was required, and none was inferred. Existing approved tools are the comparison baseline; a new integration needs a specific advantage and its own access, cost and maintenance assessment.

| Comparison | Evidence-led result | Rejected alternative and consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Product-management brainstorming versus the complete business dossier | TC01 provides real assumption and strategy reasoning, with a thinking-partner emphasis. Reference its methods and consider composition. | Do not claim strategy is missing in all existing skills or treat a discussion as a persisted accepted offer/cash decision. |
| Marketing Skills versus unmodified core adoption | TC02 contains relevant offer/pricing reasoning and truthfulness advice, but the inspected text also imposes component/score/lift and pricing heuristics. ADAPT only the justified parts. | No universal value score, assumed conversion lift or compulsory urgency enters the core. A permissive licence does not validate a heuristic. |
| Accounting plugin versus accounting execution and business economics | TC03 can assist accounting workflows; TC19 supplies an accounting-system interface, TC20 payment evidence. These are complementary scopes. | Do not turn the plugin into a certified ledger or call a month-end workflow a complete cohort-economics/growth review. |
| Restricted XLSX skill versus independently licensed tools | TC04’s local licence does not support extraction/derivatives for this proposed package. REJECT that use; TC22 and TC26 remain independent library options, with TC21 a calculation environment. | A public repository does not make the restricted skill reusable. A file library still does not become a formula evaluator merely by replacing the restricted material. |
| Exa versus Firecrawl | TC05 emphasises search/discovery and returned source context; TC06 covers extraction/search and broader interaction surfaces. Either can be adequate for an authorised reading task. | Do not require both or create a new crawler. Interaction and private-data extraction have stronger action boundaries than source reading. No relative retrieval benchmark was performed. |
| Conjoint study versus a simple response/interview collector | TC07 offers specialised choice modelling; TC08 can collect bounded responses. Choose by the evidence needed, not by software sophistication. | Do not treat a cheap interest form as paid-demand validation or require a paid conjoint study when existing records answer the decision. |
| First-party analytics versus third-party search data | TC12/TC13 describe a property or configured events; TC11 supplies index-derived research. | PostHog versus GrowthBook | TC14 combines events and analytics with experimentation; TC15 supports modular SDK assignment and warehouse-based analysis. | Mailchimp versus Customer.io | TC16 supports audience/campaign operation; TC17 distinguishes event ingress, application operations and messaging events. Either can serve an approved purpose. | Do not create a native email system or assume a POST is harmless because it looks like ingestion. No live deliverability comparison occurred. |

A source licence and a service agreement answer different questions. Root MIT licences in TC14/TC15 have enterprise and third-party boundaries; Firecrawl’s root AGPL terms do not automatically describe its hosted API or every client. TC19’s MIT API description does not licence private accounting records or free service access. No legal opinion about a particular redistribution or customer agreement is asserted here; material uncertainty routes through Stage 9 before the affected implementation.

## 3. All ten execution responsibilities stay with existing systems

A business recommendation can propose an operation but does not perform it. An operator or existing tool returns actual evidence. Read, draft and write permissions are distinct, and an external action requires authority for the specific purpose, scope and environment. A manual authorised operation or export can be the smallest adequate interface; this research does not require ten API integrations.

| ID | Original execution responsibility | Existing owner / candidates | Handoff, returned evidence and boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| ES01 | CRM operations | Appropriate existing CRM/operator; TC09 | Supply exact record, supported buyer event, allowed update and owner. Return actual state/history or error. Keep customer records in the CRM; a stage label is not proof of commitment. |
| ES02 | email delivery | Approved sending platform/operator; TC16 or TC17 | Supply reviewed message, purpose, permitted recipients and suppression/budget constraints. Return delivery and response events separately. No credentials-only sending authority or native mail infrastructure. |
| ES03 | ad buying | Authorised marketing operator and platform; TC10 | Supply accepted offer/claims, audience, spend/stop limits and measurement plan. Return actual spend/exposure/conversion definitions. A recommendation or test plan cannot spend money itself. |
| ES04 | analytics | Existing property/event system; TC12–TC14 and TC11 where relevant | Provide metric question and authorised scope. Return definitions, source settings, observations, missingness and cutoffs. Retain platform meaning rather than create a universal analytics warehouse. |
| ES05 | accounting | Existing accounting system and qualified reviewer; TC19, optional TC03 assistance | Request reviewed amounts, policy and periods, not invented treatment. Return attributed records or findings. No shadow ledger, automatic posting or certification of accounts by Business Building. |
| ES06 | payments | Authorised payment/billing provider and operator; TC20 | Supply exact accepted obligation and permitted operation/environment. Return canonical event/state, amount/date and errors. Keep charge, refund entitlement, settlement, revenue and usable cash distinct. |
| ES07 | spreadsheets | Actual spreadsheet/calculation owner; TC21, TC26; file layer TC22 | Pass Stage 7 HC01–HC08 inputs/formulas and expected reconciliations. Return computed values and input/output identity, not merely formula strings. No unapproved cloud upload or restricted-skill bundling. |
| ES08 | A/B infrastructure | Existing approved experiment system; TC14/TC15; suitable statistical calculation TC23 | Pass frozen design, exposure unit, metrics, guardrails and authority. Return actual assignment/exposure, analysis settings, validity and uncertainty. Rollout status is not business truth or a waiver of rights. |
| ES09 | landing-page hosting | Existing engineering/deployment operator; TC24 or TC25 | Supply reviewed assets and explicit environment/publication scope. Return deployment identity and served behaviour. Hosting does not create demand evidence or authority to publish an unreviewed claim. |
| ES10 | survey collection | Existing authorised research collector/operator; TC07/TC08 | Pass reviewed questions, recruitment, purpose/data limits and observation horizon. Return response and non-response context. A collection service is not a substitute for neutral design or representative evidence. |

Provider/tool boundaries include semantic identity, data custody, credentials, errors/retries, versioning and actual execution evidence. Retrying an operation requires its real idempotency rules; it cannot rely on a global “exactly once” assumption. APIs and schemas are untrusted external input for the agent’s authority model: retrieved instructions or a record field cannot expand the user’s permitted action. A rejected or failed action remains an observed failure, not a zero metric or fictitious successful mutation.

## 4. Evidenced judgement still needed

This section records Stage 10 implications, not the Stage 12 full gap matrix or Stage 13 command architecture. The inspected evidence supports a narrow native rationale: combine appropriate existing capabilities into the accepted business decision model and enforce the evidence, economics and preservation conditions that cannot be inferred merely from their outputs.

| Evidence and observed limit | Business responsibility justified for later design | What must remain reused |
|---|---|---|
| TC01/TC02 already generate strategic and commercial reasoning, but the inspected material does not demonstrate the complete accepted trace/version/gate contract; some heuristics conflict with it. | Evidence-qualified synthesis, explicit uncertainty, accepted-decision preservation and context-specific commercial choices. | Useful external reasoning and production skills; do not rebrand a whole borrowed playbook or split by book. |
| TC09 and TC16–TC20 expose state and operational events; a record or notification cannot alone prove customer progress, consent or retained value. | Interpret actual customer/buyer events and align qualification, value and obligation evidence. | CRM, support, messaging, payment and accounting state. |
| TC11–TC15 offer substantial data and analysis, but source populations, identities and attribution settings differ. | Reconcile the actual measure and inference; distinguish attribution from incrementality and a noisy metric from a supported constraint. | Collection, queries, anomaly/validity functions and appropriate statistical inference. |
| TC20’s billing metrics and TC21–TC26’s calculations need specified cost scope, retention assumptions and cash dates. | Define the economic question, reconcile bases and judge a bounded commitment against capacity/cash/quality constraints. | Formula evaluation, numerical libraries, billing and professional accounting treatment. |
| TC07/TC08 collect or model stated preferences; software sophistication does not establish representativeness or actual payment. | Select the cheapest adequate evidence, preserve the tested scope, and interpret inconclusive or conflicting observations. | Survey collection, retrieval and specialist analysis where needed. |
| Every assessed external operation has its own scope and permissions. | Consume Stage 9 constraints and route the smallest responsible correction with explicit ownership and evidence. | Qualified legal/tax conclusions and authorised execution; no autonomous executive or universal legal engine. |

The scope of these findings is the inspected material, not a claim that no existing tool anywhere can perform such judgement. A later deeper capability test may justify more reuse and a smaller native surface. Native responsibility must shrink when evidence shows an existing component meets the complete requirement; it is not protected by this shortlist.

### Bounded deterministic calculation

The calculation probe is entirely synthetic. Three engagements have consideration 120 each, recorded work of 4, 4.5 and 3.5 hours, labour at 30 per hour and an additional direct cost of 10 each. Reusing the installed Python `decimal` implementation with string inputs yields 12 hours, 360 consideration, 390 direct cost and -30 direct contribution. No acquisition cost, statutory profit or customer demand is inferred. The calculation demonstrates a sufficient existing primitive for this bounded expression, not a universal financial application or a spreadsheet-recalculation benchmark.

The checker also tests rejection of duplicate cost identities, non-finite values and non-positive ratio denominators; these are independent integrity conditions on the declared inputs. The exact temporary checker and actual run results are persisted in conformance. It does not contact customer systems or install any assessed product.
