## 1. Catalogue decision and status

All nine original candidates are assessed below in their original order. Eight are retained as independent **design candidates**; `consulting-business` is merged into an advisory mode of `professional-services`. There are no implemented or installed packs in this catalogue. Retention means a researched grammar is justified, not that improved agent or business outcomes have been measured. Core must remain adequate without a pack.

Every record identifies fit/non-fit, core overlap, specific command effects, all fourteen dimension treatments and a discriminating original synthetic case. The fourteen rows describe candidate requirements or inherited behaviour, not observed customer facts. The named sources support mechanism distinctions; the rules and consolidation are project synthesis. A future case supplies its actual customer evidence, account settings, costs, currency, dates, jurisdiction and authority.

### PK01 — `saas-business`

**Design disposition:** RETAIN AS DESIGN CANDIDATE. **Implementation status:** NOT IMPLEMENTED.

**Research basis:** R01 in the research companion.

**Fit:** Recurring access to a hosted software capability, including seat, usage or hybrid charging. Establish the actual service and commercial unit; a downloadable file, an agency calling itself SaaS or a subscription label alone is insufficient.

**Non-activation:** Do not apply to an exclusively one-time digital download or a human-delivered engagement without hosted-service economics. Store-mediated consumer subscription questions are assessed separately in PK02.

**Core/catalogue comparison:** Core already models subscriptions, value, costs and cash. The residual is a reusable account/user/meter/contract bridge and service-cost sensitivity, not another billing system. No subscription price, margin or churn threshold is inferred from R01.

| Effect | Affected commands | Required changed behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| PK01-E1 | B02, G04 | Separate user, account, payer and economic approver; request adoption and purchasing evidence at the appropriate unit; return a role-to-value and buying-path map. |
| PK01-E2 | B04, B07, E03 | Request consumed, billable and invoiced units with price/cost versions; return a metering/consideration bridge and cohort contribution, including unbilled use and support burden. |
| PK01-E3 | G06, G08, E02 | Request meaningful-use, renewal, expansion and service-quality evidence on matched horizons; assess marginal growth without replacing delivery or cash gates with MRR. |

All effects preserve the shared evidence, rights, calculation, mutation and growth invariants; they add no external authority.

| Dimension | Original dimension | Candidate treatment |
|---|---|---|

| D03 | pricing unit | Seat, account, usage or justified hybrid; distinguish value from the billing unit. |

| D07 | delivery model | Hosted operation, onboarding, integration and support dependencies remain operator-owned. |
| D08 | retention model | Account and revenue cohorts, voluntary renewal, usage and expansion stay distinct. |
| D09 | unit economics | Include marginal infrastructure/usage and support; expose cost changes at higher consumption. |

**Design disposition:** RETAIN AS DESIGN CANDIDATE. **Implementation status:** NOT IMPLEMENTED.

**Research basis:** R02, R03 in the research companion.

**Fit:** Consumer recurring or prepaid access delivered through a mobile application where store-managed plans, payment or entitlement behaviour materially affects the business decision. Verify actual purchase channel and applicable configuration.

**Non-activation:** A mobile interface to a direct-billed service with no relevant store mechanics does not automatically need this grammar. A prepaid arrangement must not be treated as auto-renewing merely because it is listed under subscriptions.

**Core/catalogue comparison:** PK01 covers generic recurring software, but store offers, restored access, grace/recovery and channel-specific cash need an explicit consumer entitlement model. Retain independently useful mobile detail without importing platform SDKs or universal commission rates.

| PK02-E3 | B07, E03, G08 | Request gross customer payment, adjustments, platform charges, net proceeds and settlement dates; check remaining service/refund obligations before paid acquisition. |

### PK04 — `consulting-business`

**Design disposition:** MERGE INTO PROFESSIONAL-SERVICES ADVISORY MODE. **Implementation status:** NOT IMPLEMENTED.

**Research basis:** R04, R05, R06, R07 in the research companion.

**Fit:** Advice, diagnosis or knowledge transfer is the bounded engagement outcome, and client action materially mediates realised impact. Select the advisory mode only when these facts are relevant.

**Non-activation:** A consultant title alone does not activate the mode. Standard execution work can use PK03 without extra advisory gates. Neither this name nor a client signature grants regulated-advice authority.

**Core/catalogue comparison:** The additional responsibilities are client problem framing, sponsorship, adopted recommendations and knowledge transfer. They fit PK03 inputs and acceptance fields without incompatible pricing/cash assumptions. Retain the seed and distinct checks as a mode, not a duplicated independently installed pack or a hidden parent dependency.

| Effect | Affected commands | Required changed behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| PK04-E1 | B03, G04 | Separate diagnostic question, sponsor expectations, recommendation deliverable and client decision; define what the engagement can promise versus what depends on client implementation. |
| PK04-E2 | B06, E02 | Request knowledge-transfer and client acceptance evidence; distinguish a delivered report, accepted advice, implemented action and achieved benefit. |
| PK04-E3 | B09, E04, E07 | Test whether the advice can change the decision and whether the client can act; preserve inconclusive impact rather than inventing attributed ROI or escalating into broad transformation. |

All effects preserve the shared evidence, rights, calculation, mutation and growth invariants; they add no external authority.

| Dimension | Original dimension | Candidate treatment |
|---|---|---|

| D03 | pricing unit | Inherit PK03 options; outcome-fee conditions must distinguish controllable from client-dependent work. |
| D04 | money model | Inherit PK03 exchange model; no extra revenue line merely because advice is strategic. |
| D05 | channel priorities | Emphasise evidence of relevant expertise and trusted introductions only when audience fit supports it. |
| D06 | sales path | Add problem sponsorship and decision/implementation readiness to service qualification. |
| D07 | delivery model | Add discovery access, evidence review, recommendation acceptance and transfer responsibilities. |
| D08 | retention model | Follow-on work requires a new client need; dependency creation is not a retention mechanism. |
| D09 | unit economics | Inherit service costing, adding discovery, review and transfer effort rather than ignoring it. |
| D10 | capacity constraints | Inherit human resource constraints; client review and stakeholder access also bound feasibility. |
| D11 | cash behaviour | Inherit dated collections; acceptance-dependent milestones remain conditional until evidenced. |
| D12 | core metrics | Separate recommendation delivery, acceptance, adoption and attributed impact; no assumed ROI. |
| D13 | experiment types | Bounded diagnostic or decision workshop with stated possible outcomes and client dependencies. |
| D14 | quality criteria | Competence, independence, confidentiality and usable transfer, without claiming professional certification. |

**Discriminating design case S04:** A delivered report does not establish client acceptance, adoption or economic improvement. **Design disposition:** RETAIN AS DESIGN CANDIDATE. **Implementation status:** NOT IMPLEMENTED.

**Fit:** The business sells physical goods with material stock, fulfilment, returns or supplier commitments. Establish ownership, fulfilment arrangement and actual stock states, including outsourced delivery.

**Core/catalogue comparison:** Core includes costs and cash; the reusable residual is a SKU/order/stock-state bridge, fulfilment and reverse-flow exposure. Provider inventory grades cannot replace contribution, available stock or funding checks.

| PK05-E3 | G01, G08, E06 | Assess acquisition against inventory and working-capital constraints; use demand and dated obligations before proposing replenishment, not a revenue grade or universal stock target. |

All effects preserve the shared evidence, rights, calculation, mutation and growth invariants; they add no external authority.

### PK06 — `marketplace-business`

**Design disposition:** RETAIN AS DESIGN CANDIDATE. **Implementation status:** NOT IMPLEMENTED.

**Research basis:** R09, R10 in the research companion.

**Fit:** The business intermediates distinct sides whose matching or transaction progress and incentives materially determine value. Establish the actual roles, transaction/funds flows and the relevant local or category market.

**Non-activation:** A direct seller with its own stock is not a marketplace merely because its website lists many products. Interest from suppliers alone is not buyer demand or demonstrated matching liquidity.

**Core/catalogue comparison:** Core models roles and economics; the reusable residual is side-specific outcomes and constrained matching with own-fee/funds reconciliation. R09 supplies a useful two-sided distinction but its inverted rate wording is explicitly rejected.

| Effect | Affected commands | Required changed behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| PK06-E1 | B02, G01, G04 | Request each side’s progress, alternatives, eligibility and available capacity by relevant market/window; return separate qualification and acquisition hypotheses. |
| PK06-E2 | B05, B07, E03 | Separate total transaction value, own consideration, seller funds, reserves, chargebacks and settlement responsibilities using current reviewed definitions. |
| PK06-E3 | G06, G08, E02 | Measure eligible requests successfully matched and eligible supply used on stated horizons, including failures and time-to-match; do not infer network effects from registrations or total GMV. |

All effects preserve the shared evidence, rights, calculation, mutation and growth invariants; they add no external authority.

| Dimension | Original dimension | Candidate treatment |
|---|---|---|
| D01 | customer / buyer structure | Buyer, supplier, intermediary, payer and beneficiary roles are explicit on each side. |
| D02 | offer grammar | Matching/service promise and counterparty duties are separated; no guaranteed liquidity. |
| D03 | pricing unit | Transaction fee, subscription, lead fee or justified combination with a clear payer. |
| D04 | money model | Transaction value differs from own fees; each additional exchange has costs and obligations. |
| D05 | channel priorities | Acquire the side and market implicated by evidence, not both sides indiscriminately. |
| D06 | sales path | Qualification, search/request, match, agreement, completion and dispute remain separate. |
| D07 | delivery model | Matching and counterparty delivery depend on actual eligible supply and trust/recovery work. |
| D08 | retention model | Repeat value and leakage are side-specific; listings and paid renewals have different meanings. |
| D09 | unit economics | Own contribution includes support, incentives, disputes and payment exposure; not GMV margin. |
| D10 | capacity constraints | Local/category/time supply, demand and moderation/support constrain transaction commitments. |
| D11 | cash behaviour | Third-party funds and reserves are not available growth cash; settlement/refund dates matter. |
| D12 | core metrics | Matched eligible requests / eligible requests; supplied participation and time-to-match separately. |
| D13 | experiment types | Bounded manual match or market-cell test with genuine participants and explicit authority. |
| D14 | quality criteria | Match relevance, completion, counterparty quality and rights; no fake listings or social proof. |

**Discriminating design case S06:** Four matches from ten eligible requests is 40%, not 250%. Keep unmet demand and third-party funds visible; a high transaction value does not establish platform revenue or permission to subsidise growth. Exact inputs/prompts and the actual bounded local comparisons are in the [design probes](2026-09-10-stage-14-design-probes.md). They do not establish installed-pack performance.

| D09 | unit economics | Include the full work cycle and transport/material costs, not only hands-on minutes. |
| D10 | capacity constraints | Window, route, facility/equipment and staff constraints all matter beyond aggregate hours. |
| D11 | cash behaviour | Deposits, supplier/fuel outlay, no-shows and collection delays enter dated obligations. |
| D12 | core metrics | Door-to-door contribution, serviceable conversion, attendance and complete resource minutes. |
| D13 | experiment types | Bounded service-area or booking/attendance test, not immediate geographic expansion. |
| D14 | quality criteria | Reliable arrival/completion, safe competent work, clear terms and legitimate customer exit. |

**Discriminating design case S09:** Five profitable visits can exceed the day once travel and setup are included. A nominal time bound is not a verified route or schedule; preserve the offer while reviewing intake or the service area. Exact inputs/prompts and the actual bounded local comparisons are in the [design probes](2026-09-10-stage-14-design-probes.md). They do not establish installed-pack performance.

## 3. Composition and review decisions

The selected grammar is tied to a bounded exchange, not inferred from an organisation's headline. A business selling software and consultancy can use scoped SaaS and services views without adding their revenues or costs twice. A creator selling physical merchandise and digital access needs explicit component promises and shared cost identities. A local-service marketplace separates intermediary matching from a provider's visit capacity. No candidate overrides facts, applicable constraints, accepted decisions or core invariants.

The advisory mode is fully described here rather than deferred as an unexamined candidate. Its knowledge-transfer, sponsor and adoption questions remain mandatory when the selected mode applies; merging is not deleting those responsibilities. All other overlaps are retained only where the distinct mechanism changes reusable questions, metrics or acceptance. Do not install every candidate as a default business stack.

Each candidate is reviewed when its mechanism assumptions, core contracts, relevant source conditions or demonstrated behaviour change. The current source evidence does not select a universally optimal provider, price, channel, legal treatment or implementation sequence. More adequate core behaviour can reduce the pack surface. Actual pack implementation and installed comparisons belong to their assigned later stages and must not be inferred from catalogue presence.
