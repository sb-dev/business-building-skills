# Stage 17: Authored-control calibration and semantic review

**Date:** 12 September 2026  
**Method:** Direct assistant review of the actual Stage 17 corpus and fixture documents against original §24 and the dimension-specific anchors.  
**Evidence type:** Review of authored synthetic controls, not an installed-agent run, independent expert judgement, measured model accuracy or a live business experiment.

## 1. Procedure and limits

After the full fixture/candidate texts were written, read each actual JSON input, question, failing candidate, adequate candidate and assigned dimensions from the stored corpus. Compare the literal claim with its supplied evidence and the relevant rubric. Check numeric witnesses using the separate executed verifier. Retain the accepted parent examples/stress cases and their source identities. Evaluate the bounded claim review requested by each micro-case; these short controls are not presented as complete outputs for a Level 5 business thesis.

The table reports each applied dimension separately. FAIL in the old-candidate column means the authored bad reasoning is detected; PASS in the adequate-candidate column means that narrowly scoped repair respects the stated criterion. This is not a business viability score or proof that an installed skill can generate that answer. Unassessed wider business dimensions remain outside the micro-case claim; a real scale recommendation still needs every relevant independent gate. A correct blocked commercial conclusion is permitted.

The grader knows the controls were authored; this is not blinded model assessment. Its value is an explicit, reviewable demonstration that the proposed criteria distinguish the supplied reasoning. Production benchmarks must retain actual outputs, tool evidence, configuration and grader provenance under the case contract. Their results cannot be inferred from this record.
