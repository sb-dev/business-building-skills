### A01 — growth recommendation ignores churn

**Exact overlay and review request:**

```text
Synthetic overlay A01 for ST16-A-v1. This is a separate review subject; preserve the accepted base and reset it before any other overlay.
{
  "candidate_memo": "Closing paid accounts rose from 200 to 240, so retention is healthy and acquisition should scale."
}
Audit the memo against the unchanged due cohort. State which observations are valid and which inference fails.
```

**Failing candidate (authored negative control):** Twenty per cent account growth proves strong retention; continue acquisition without further review.

**Required discriminating findings:**

- 140 of 200 old accounts paid: 70%; 60 did not renew in paid status. Ten grace accounts are a subset of the unpaid 60, not an additional ten losses.
- Closing paid count 240 combines 140 old and 100 new; it cannot identify old-cohort retention or causal improvement.
- Review use is 100/200=50%; the cause of non-renewal remains unknown. Cash and economic gates also remain adverse.

**Adequate candidate (authored comparison control):** The closing paid count increased, while the old paid-renewal rate is 70%. Keep cause uncertain, audit cohort definitions and propose a bounded retained-value investigation; do not clear scale.

**Smallest responsible repair:** Repair the retention interpretation and affected scale recommendation. Preserve price, offer, refund promise, customer hypothesis and source observations; do not force an app or brand redesign.

**Protected versions:** `monthly-6-v1`; `no-free-trial-v1`; `30-day-money-back-v1`; `budget-review-assistance-not-investment-advice`.

**Handoff requirements:** Research/operator: due-cohort event definitions, cancellation reasons and lawful source access; no new collection authorised.

**Especially implicated independent gates:** GG01, GG05, GG06. All other relevant base gates remain in force.

## 7. Bounded evidence programme

The following is a complete next-evidence design. No recruitment, customer test or external action has occurred. Numeric recruitment or inference requirements belong to a later authorised study if its decision requires them; the finite record audits and qualitative diagnostics below make no statistical claim.

| Stage 8 experiment field | Case-specific design |
|---|---|
| assumption | Users can understand the accepted monthly price, renewal and refund promise before purchase; this is a prerequisite to an informed exchange, not a test of financial savings. |
| hypothesis | The exact pre-purchase presentation enables each observed participant to correctly explain the price, recurrence, no-trial state and remedy without correction. |
| cheapest valid test | First inspect the text/flow and reconcile known defects; then propose a disclosed task walkthrough. An opinion poll cannot test task comprehension, and a paid acquisition campaign is unnecessary for this question. |
| target population | Future authorised adult prospective users/payers matching the declared customer hypothesis; no contacts or private records are supplied or authorised by this design. |
| success failure signal | Record each material misunderstanding and exact task. A recurring-term or remedy misunderstanding challenges readiness; observed understanding supports only the tested presentation/population, never population prevalence or conversion. |
| guardrails | Design only here. Before real exposure obtain owner authority, applicable privacy/consumer findings, a finite recruitment/work cap and usable funding. No charge, real financial-data upload, unsupported benefit claim or change to existing rights. |
| duration sample | Complete the full purchase-decision task before interpreting it. The future operator must justify recruitment and information needs against a bounded comprehension decision; this qualitative diagnostic plan cannot support a statistical success-rate claim. |
| confounders | Familiar participants, prompted answers, moderator coaching, changed wording, channel differences and missing cancellation/refund behaviour; retain unsuccessful observations. |
| decision rule | Known contradiction: propose local repair first. Material misunderstanding: revise implicated presentation and retest. Adequate observed understanding: request only the next bounded purchase/value test. Missing/invalid records: no readiness conclusion. Inconclusive: retain baseline and review the information need. Guardrail breach: stop affected exposure through its authorised owner. |

The learning record must preserve the hypothesis, this unexecuted design, supplied fictional observations, actual deterministic results, qualified interpretation, bounded decision, remaining unknowns and proposed next commitment.
