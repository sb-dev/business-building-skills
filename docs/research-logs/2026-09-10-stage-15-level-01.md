**Level:** 1  
**Capability trace:** C04, C05, C11, C15, C17, C18, C22

**Optional mechanism fit:** professional-services; not required.

**Purpose and depth:** Compare two price structures for the same bounded reporting job without redesigning the offer.

A small reporting service is considering a fixed fee or time-based price for the SAME single-report scope. The three completed-job times are fictional records, not a forecast distribution. Scope and intended customer are accepted only within this fixture; both prices are proposals.

Task:
Compare the two structures at every observed workload using actual deterministic arithmetic. State the price unit, included work, acceptance, variation and payment disclosures. Separate valued owner labour from a cash payment. Identify the fixed-fee labour boundary without converting it into a promised service limit or an observed price preference. Recommend a bounded option or test, not an offer or customer rewrite. ```

### Expected artefacts

A price-options record, executed calculation record with units/inputs, and one bounded test/decision note. Do not create unrelated outputs or replace source evidence with the assessment. A blocked commitment can still have a complete, useful design or review record.

### Discriminating acceptance

Fixed-fee contribution at 2/3/5 hours is 50/20/-40; hourly consideration is 80/120/200 and contribution 10/20/40. The fixed-fee zero-contribution boundary is 11/3 hours under these assumptions. There is no demonstrated willingness-to-pay winner. A valid answer may prefer hourly or an explicitly bounded fixed scope pending buyer evidence. The verifier checks the fixed arithmetic; direct review must also examine evidence scope, causal limits and the requested business reasoning. A favourable calculation alone does not pass all growth gates.

### Negative control

Fail a recommendation that uses the three-hour case alone, silently caps the accepted scope, guarantees profitability, or presents a hypothetical buyer preference as measured.

### Preservation and smallest responsible repair

Keep small-agency-v1 and one-report-v1. Record any price/scope revision as a proposal requiring approval; no quote is sent.
