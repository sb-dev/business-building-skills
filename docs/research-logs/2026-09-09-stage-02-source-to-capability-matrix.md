**Date:** 9 September 2026

## Reading the matrix

Source IDs and one-based PDF locators resolve through the [corpus register](2026-09-09-stage-02-five-book-capability-corpus.md). Capability IDs resolve through the [provisional taxonomy](2026-09-09-stage-02-reconciliation-and-taxonomy.md). Row IDs identify source candidates, not skills or commands. Grouping research by source does not define the production architecture.

The **Concept** column attributes a source idea, preserving its identifiable terminology. **Capability, Inputs, Output, Metric/evidence and Risks** express the project's interpretation and proposed checks unless expressly described as a source definition. These are Stage 2 workflow/evaluation implications, not implemented contracts or measured results. **Overlap** names related sources; it is not a vote count or proof of independent corroboration. Every inferred responsibility stays within Stage 1's advisory and professional boundary.

Disposition means: **retain** as a bounded candidate; **merge** a duplicated responsibility; **adapt** a useful idea whose scope or safeguards must change; **reject** the stated tactic or universal rule, not the whole book; **research** a claim whose decision value remains unresolved. None means empirically validated. Ordinary-language capability labels are provisional project vocabulary, not claimed professional equivalents established by research.

## Boundary and traceability

These candidates support business decisions and evidence requirements. They do not create a CRM, ad platform, accounting system, legal-advice skill or autonomous executive authority. Rejected tactics remain visible as evidence of source tension; they are not installable behaviours. The taxonomy supplies production responsibilities, workflow implications, evaluation criteria and future case seeds without claiming Stage 13 commands or Stage 17 benchmarks already exist.

No fixed row count or equal source allocation was imposed by the specification. Completeness is checked against all five dossiers, main capability families, all prescribed capture fields, the initial capability hypothesis and the explicitly required comparisons. [Conformance](2026-09-09-stage-02-conformance.md) records the actual row count and reference checks.
