## Interpretation and grading boundary

Separate three questions. First, did the run actually execute with the right installation, inputs and authority? Second, are its numbers and record identities correct? Third, does the actual output reason adequately about the case? A structurally complete persuasive document can fail the third question. A responsible business decision to stop or seek evidence can pass response conformance.

For every relevant reasoning/behaviour dimension, record PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED or NOT APPLICABLE with an actual output/evidence locator and explanation. PASS means the bounded response meets that dimension's contract; it does not mean the business should grow. FAIL identifies a violated requirement. BLOCKED identifies unavailable evaluation evidence or a dependency that prevents assessing the response. NOT APPLICABLE requires a specific scope justification and cannot conceal a relevant unknown. Preserve the business's own GG01–GG07 and interpretation/next-decision findings separately. If the business correctly reports missing cash evidence, the response can PASS appropriate uncertainty handling while its cash-dependent commercial commitment remains BLOCKED.

Do not average the sixteen business dimensions into a startup or opportunity score. A case-conformance gate can require all mandatory obligations without turning it into a single measure of business quality. Fabricated evidence, misleading terms, overridden rights, unauthorised effects, known unfunded/overloaded scale approval, or an ungrounded broad rewrite are critical failures. Good prose, other dimensions and conversion estimates cannot offset them.

The grader receives the frozen subject input, actual response/tool traces and separate oracle. It cites specific claims and supporting/contradicting facts, checks calculation evidence, recognises permitted alternatives, and considers whether a failure is local or disqualifies the requested commitment. Evaluate meaning; do not use required-word counts, output length, similarity to a reference paragraph or keyword spotting as semantic proof. Business tasks admit multiple adequate strategies and legitimate inconclusive outcomes. Adjudicate genuine disagreement against the exact facts and requirement, recording both assessments; do not secretly move the goalposts. An LLM grader is an evaluation aid with recorded identity/configuration, not independent professional authority.

Use opaque condition labels when feasible for paired assessment and do not reveal which arm should win. Keep expected findings and authored adequate candidates outside subject prompts. All mandatory cases and failed/aborted attempts remain in the record. A retry is a separate attempt with a reason, never a replacement that erases the first result. If making a broader reliability or superiority claim, predefine a justified repeat/sample/analysis plan; one paired case only establishes the observed case-specific difference. No unmeasured token saving, economic improvement or success rate is inferred.

### Complete pack coverage and shared conditions

All eight retained independent pack candidates and the accepted advisory mode have a concrete S01–S09 design probe below. The seven obligations above apply to every implemented pack, not merely a representative passing pack. Their current status remains NOT IMPLEMENTED. Actual paired runs, activation and installation evidence belong to the later implementation/validation stages; this design specifies them completely without claiming they occurred.

For each comparison hold the task, fixed data, accepted decisions, core version, host/settings, permitted tools and output requirements constant. The only declared treatment is selection of the exact pack/version (or the advisory mode within professional-services). Use a fresh isolated output context for each arm. Retain both actual outputs, tool evidence, effect-level findings, unchanged invariants and any confounder. Core receives all business facts and safeguards; a pack cannot win by depriving core of mandatory knowledge. Correct identical decisions are allowed. More systematic specialised decomposition may be an observed benefit, but a longer answer is not itself one.

The positive condition uses actual mechanism facts plus explicit selection. The no-selection condition keeps the task adequate under core. The incompatible condition applies the recorded non-fit facts. The precedence condition supplies a proposed pack default conflicting with a clearly identified accepted fact/decision; it must be rejected within that scope. Top-level factual/legal conflicts remain unresolved rather than choosing whichever favours growth. For all conditions test actual behaviour, not only manifest structure.

A stage-17 design record cannot satisfy later authored-pack execution or measured-comparison requirements.

### Preservation, repair and worked rehearsal

Retain every accepted source version before the review. An evaluator writes an assessment; it never silently edits its subject. Compare no change, a local repair, a bounded evidence test and any proposed coupled repair. Require a fact-based explanation for every changed layer and a disposition for each genuinely affected dependency. Correct arithmetic does not make a broad customer/offer/price/delivery/brand rewrite proportionate.

The AD09 fixture is a small synthetic rehearsal of the acquisition-quality failure family also covered by accepted E02/E10. Its authored old answer selects channel A from cost per lead, despite CAC 100 and after-acquisition contribution -80, while B has CAC 20 and residual 50. The corrected authored answer recognises those differences without asserting causal superiority or safe scale. The separate calibration record applies R08/R09/R13 to both texts and records why the old text fails and the repaired text passes this bounded claim review. This rehearses evaluator discrimination; it is not an escaped installed-agent defect or a repaired installed-skill run.

When a real escape occurs, RG01–RG07 require actual captured/replayed old and repaired evidence. Preserve the full E02/E10 parent cases alongside the minimal regression, rerun the affected calculation, channel, qualification, economics and preservation checks, and widen downstream review only where the changed layer has real dependencies. If the defect is a calculation or grader error, repair that owner instead of rewriting business facts or skill policy. Keep the old evidence and the new version permanently; release/maturity gates remain closed while mandatory failures or blocked evaluation evidence remain unresolved.
