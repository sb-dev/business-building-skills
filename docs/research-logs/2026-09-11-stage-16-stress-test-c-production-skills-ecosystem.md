## 4. Positive control

**Proposal:** Preserve the useful open core and existing rights, continue only evidenced paid services, use restricted sponsorship only for maintenance, and test one additional paid-support hypothesis before adding other revenue lines.

**Expected result:** **PASS** — The response keeps stakeholder incentives and funding/capacity evidence separate while sequencing one reversible commercial test.

The positive control sequences one evidence-generating paid-support test while keeping the core usable, sponsorship restricted, contributors voluntary and current services within their explicit scope. It does not pre-approve a marketplace or multiple simultaneous revenue lines.

## 6. Deterministic baseline findings

- Observed paid services are `3 × £1,000 = £3,000`; stars and downloads are not substituted for paid customers.
- Maintenance value is `30 × £40 = £1,200`; £600 restricted sponsorship leaves a £600 maintenance-funding gap.
- Total work is `30 + 36 + 10 + 8 = 84` hours versus 80 available: a four-hour overload.
- Total valued work plus hosting is `84 × £40 + £240 = £3,600`.
- Services plus restricted sponsorship also total £3,600, but equality does not create spare capacity or unrestricted cash.
- Before the eligible maintenance settlement, total cash is £4,600, of which £600 is restricted and £4,000 usable. Paying the £400 eligible maintenance invoice leaves £4,200 total, £200 restricted and still £4,000 usable.
- Paid-support subscribers observed: zero; marketplace supply/buyer transaction evidence: absent.

These are fixture arithmetic/identity results only. They do not prove commercial viability, customer benefit, lawful execution or generalisation to a live business.

## 7. Preservation and failure interpretation

A stress case does not earn a pass by rewriting the baseline until the shortcut becomes acceptable. A correct response retains contrary evidence, blocks or fails only the implicated commitment, and preserves unrelated accepted decisions. If an external action would be required to test the repair, this design names the handoff but does not perform the action.
