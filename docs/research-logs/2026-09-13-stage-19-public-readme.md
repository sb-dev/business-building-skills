# Business Building Skills

Turn business ideas and existing businesses into evidence-backed offers, acquisition systems and viable economic models.

For founders, independent operators and teams working on consumer or B2B businesses. Start with the decision you need to make: what to offer, how to price it, which customers to reach, whether you can deliver it, or what needs to change. Build only the records that help that decision.

**Design preview:** the specifications and synthetic exercises are available to read and try. Installable skills and Extension Packs are not yet available. The exercises let you explore the workflow with your own assistant; they do not demonstrate installed product performance or business success.

## What business work it can perform

The design covers a connected business-building loop:

| Your question | Useful output |
|---|---|
| Whose problem matters, and what do we know? | Customer/problem and value account, with observations and assumptions separated |
| What can we credibly sell, at what price? | Offer, pricing options and money model with explicit scope, terms and costs |
| How could suitable customers find and buy it? | Channel hypothesis, qualification criteria and a coherent sales path |
| Can we fulfil the promise and keep creating value? | Delivery, capacity, retention, expansion and referral proposals |
| Do the economics and cash timing support the decision? | Reproducible contribution calculations, workload checks and dated cash scenarios |
| What should we learn or change next? | Bounded experiment, learning record, constraint diagnosis and smallest justified repair |

Use an existing business’s accepted decisions and evidence when available. A new venture can begin with explicit unknowns. Neither needs to follow every framework or maximise growth at the expense of the owner’s actual objective.

## Evidence, experiments and commitments

Keep the observation, metric, interpretation, hypothesis and decision distinct. A generated customer profile is a hypothesis; an interview is an attributed account; a payment is evidence of payment on those terms. None automatically proves a market or continuing customer value.

Begin with the material uncertainty behind the decision. Use existing evidence when it resolves the question. Otherwise choose the cheapest adequate test and write its hypothesis, target population, method, success and adverse signals, guardrails, duration, confounders and decision rule before collecting results. Record what actually happened, what remains unknown and the next bounded commitment. An inconclusive result stays inconclusive.

Approval records permission to take a scoped action. Validation records what the evidence supports. Before more acquisition, hiring or inventory, separately check customer value, downstream lead quality, delivery capacity, contribution, retained value, dated usable cash and applicable constraints. Passing one check cannot make the others disappear.

## Truthful growth

Make the offer compelling through useful value, clear scope, credible proof and deliverable promises. Price, payment, renewal, cancellation and refund terms must be visible and consistent with actual behaviour. Scarcity and urgency need a real basis. Fabricated testimonials, unsupported guarantees or ROI claims, deceptive comparisons, hidden terms, dark patterns and spam fail the business-quality standard.

Judge acquisition through suitable customers and downstream value, costs and retention. More leads, billing or revenue alone cannot justify scale. Preserve customer rights and supported decisions when repairing the responsible part of the business.

## Quick start

Try one bounded pricing decision. Use an assistant that can read the prompt, write local case outputs and execute arithmetic with a calculator or code. If a capability is unavailable, keep the missing execution explicit; a prose estimate is not a calculation receipt.

1. Start a fresh conversation or case workspace and copy the complete prompt below. It contains all the facts needed for this exercise.
2. Ask the assistant to analyse the supplied case. The named skills and commands are logical task selectors describing the intended responsibility; they are not shell commands and do not activate an installed skill in this preview.
3. Inspect the price-options record, actual calculation record and bounded test/decision note. Keep the supplied source facts separate from the new outputs.

```text
All facts, identities, prices, permissions and results below are synthetic. Named projects are rehearsal labels, not statements about their actual state. Use core behaviour without requiring an Extension Pack. Work only in a new local case-output directory: analysis, deterministic calculations and the requested draft artefacts are authorised. Do not contact people, send messages, spend money, charge, publish, mutate customer systems, change accepted source records or give professional legal/tax/investment conclusions. Keep missing evidence unknown and supplied accepted versions intact. Record the actual calculation method/results; never invent an executed experiment or installed-agent result.

A small reporting service is considering a fixed fee or time-based price for the SAME single-report scope. The three completed-job times are fictional records, not a forecast distribution. Scope and intended customer are accepted only within this fixture; both prices are proposals.

Use these logical selectors as needed: business-build/design-pricing, business-evaluate/evaluate-component [pricing].

Facts (JSON):
{
  "synthetic": true,
  "case_version": "E01-v1",
  "currency": "GBP",
  "accepted_versions": {
    "customer": "small-agency-v1",
    "offer": "one-report-v1"
  },
  "observations": {
    "past_job_hours": [
      "2",
      "3",
      "5"
    ],
    "labour_value_per_hour": "30",
    "other_direct_cost_per_job": "10"
  },
  "scenarios": {
    "fixed_fee": "120",
    "hourly_price": "40"
  },
  "unknowns": [
    "Willingness to pay under either new structure",
    "Frequency of each workload at future volume"
  ]
}

Task:
Compare the two structures at every observed workload using actual deterministic arithmetic. State the price unit, included work, acceptance, variation and payment disclosures. Separate valued owner labour from a cash payment. Identify the fixed-fee labour boundary without converting it into a promised service limit or an observed price preference. Recommend a bounded option or test, not an offer or customer rewrite. Specify a cheap price/scope-comprehension or quote test with a decision-changing signal, adverse and inconclusive outcomes, and permission still needed before contacting anyone. Review the proposal in pricing mode without editing its source.
```

Check your result after trying the task:

| Recorded work | Fixed-fee consideration | Fixed-fee contribution | Hourly consideration | Hourly contribution |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 hours | £120 | £50 | £80 | £10 |
| 3 hours | £120 | £20 | £120 | £20 |
| 5 hours | £120 | -£40 | £200 | £40 |

Here contribution means consideration less valued labour and the £10 other direct cost. Fixed-fee contribution is `120 − 30 × hours − 10`; hourly contribution is `40 × hours − 30 × hours − 10`. The fixed-fee zero-contribution boundary is exactly `11/3` hours. It does not amend the accepted scope. Valuing owner labour does not establish a cash payment, and these numbers are not net profit or a cash forecast.

A useful answer preserves the customer and report scope, recognises the five-hour loss under the fixed fee and leaves willingness to pay unknown. It proposes a small, honest comprehension or quote test with different actions for supportive, adverse and inconclusive findings. Customer contact still needs permission. The three fictional job times do not establish a future workload distribution or a price winner.

## Project structure grows with the business work

Start your own business work in one ordinary dossier. Give it an owner, objective, bounded decision, version/date, relevant context and existing obligations. Keep a compact business map, evidence and unknowns, the relevant offer/economics, and a decision note together. A pricing comparison does not need a CRM or a new company-wide documentation system.

| When the work grows | Add only what now needs independent review |
|---|---|
| One decision | An inline calculation and decision note in the dossier |
| One commercial component | A linked offer, channel or retention record with its evidence and terms |
| A whole business | Connected customer, value, offer, sales, delivery, economics and cash records |
| Repeated experiments or multiple owners | Separate experiment and learning records, evidence locators and versioned decisions |
| A repair | A change proposal identifying the responsible layer, preserved versions and affected checks |

Use links to authorised source records. Keep private customer data and credentials out of the public repository. Splitting a file changes its organisation, not its evidence or approval state. The [workflow specification](../02-business-building-skills-workflows-and-artifacts-spec.md#1-operating-procedure-and-minimal-records) defines the record meanings.

## Core skills

The design selects four capability-based skills. Each must be useful independently; no pack, sibling skill or Pactwright runtime is required.

| Skill | Responsibility | Example selectors |
|---|---|---|
| `business-build` | Construct or explicitly revise the relevant business component; identify assumptions and record learning | `design-offer`, `design-pricing`, `design-experiment` |
| `business-grow` | Propose qualified acquisition, sales, retention, referral and expansion within evidence, economics and capacity | `select-channel`, `design-acquisition`, `design-retention` |
| `business-evaluate` | Assess supplied evidence and artefacts, diagnose constraints and recommend a bounded change in a separate assessment | `evaluate-component`, `evaluate-unit-economics`, `recommend-smallest-change` |
| `business-pack-author` | Research, author and evaluate a reusable business-model specialisation | `define-specialisation`, `build-showcase`, `compare-core-vs-pack` |

The portable request form is `Use business-build / design-offer with this dossier`. The [command contracts](../03-business-building-skills-repository-and-contracts-spec.md#5-common-command-interface-and-routing) define all 32 selectors, their inputs and allowed changes. `design-acquisition` chooses lead-magnet, outreach or content-loop mode. `evaluate-component` chooses opportunity, offer, pricing, money-model, channel, funnel or retention; `evaluate-unit-economics` chooses economics, cash or both. An evaluation writes an assessment and preserves its subject. A selector grants no permission to send, spend or publish.

## Extension Packs

Packs specialise reusable business mechanisms: who buys and pays, what is delivered, how it is priced, which costs and obligations matter, and how to evaluate it. An industry or location label alone is insufficient.

The [catalogue](../06-business-building-extension-pack-catalogue.md#1-selection-and-use) contains eight independent design candidates, all **not implemented**:

| Candidate | What changes |
|---|---|
| `saas-business` | Account/user/payer roles, metered service economics and retention |
| `consumer-mobile-subscription` | Store-mediated payment, entitlement, renewal and settlement |
| `professional-services` | Engagement scope, shared human capacity, acceptance and collection; includes an advisory mode for advice, adoption and client-mediated impact |
| `ecommerce-business` | Owned stock, fulfilment, returns and working capital |
| `marketplace-business` | Each side’s matching needs, own fees and counterparty funds |
| `creator-digital-product` | Creation and update effort, edition access and continuing promises |
| `open-source-commercialisation` | Adopter, contributor, maintainer, sponsor and buyer incentives; rights and funding |
| `local-service-business` | Territory, visits, travel/setup and appointment capacity |

Select a pack explicitly when its mechanism fits. Verified applicable legal constraints and explicit project facts/instructions constrain approved business decisions; those decisions outrank pack defaults, which outrank core defaults. Conflicts need resolution, and mandatory truthfulness, evidence and rights rules always apply. Use one primary pack per bounded exchange by default; hybrids must reconcile shared costs and obligations once. Advisory work is a mode of professional services, not a ninth installation.

Pack authoring must show why existing behaviour is insufficient, define concrete changes, supply an exact showcase prompt and positive/negative cases, and actually compare core with core plus pack before claiming an implemented difference. See the [pack contract](../05-business-building-customisation-packs-spec.md).

## Execution tools

Business Building Skills owns the business judgement and requested evidence. Use capable existing systems for execution:

| Work | Execution responsibility |
|---|---|
| Arithmetic, economics and dated cash | Calculator or recalculating spreadsheet with recorded inputs, formulas, units and results |
| Market/customer research and surveys | Authorised research and collection tools with source dates and limitations |
| CRM, analytics and experiment assignment | The actual source system, preserving event definitions, cohorts and permissions |
| Email, advertising, billing and accounting | The authorised operator and appropriate existing service |
| Product, design, research, legal or media deliverables | The relevant Production Skills or qualified specialist |

A supplied export may be enough; no integration is required merely to think through a case. A tool handoff states the operation, target, purpose, permitted scope and expected return evidence. Record what actually happened. Reconcile an uncertain external effect before retrying it. Tool availability, a completed analysis or an approved business plan does not itself authorise an external action.

## Evaluation and benchmark

The [benchmark specification](../04-testing-and-benchmark-spec.md) separates executed calculations and repository checks from business reasoning and behaviour. It defines 15 progressive cases, three canonical stress structures, adversarial cases, pack comparisons and regression requirements. The published evidence at this revision covers design review and deterministic checks; installed-agent benchmarks, measured pack comparisons and clean installation have not been completed.

Assess customer evidence, problem importance, value, offer, truthfulness, pricing, monetisation, channel fit, lead quality, sales path, delivery, retention, unit economics, cash, experiment quality and diagnosis separately. There is no single startup score. A response that correctly identifies an unfunded cash gap can pass evaluation while the proposed business expansion remains blocked.

Reproducible runs must retain the exact prompt and inputs, actual host/core/pack versions, outputs, tool results and per-dimension assessment. Keep expected answers separate from subject inputs. Test a pack’s activation, non-activation, precedence, changed behaviour, metrics, incompatible cases and actual core-versus-pack difference. Retain escaped defects as regressions, showing the old failure and repaired behaviour without weakening the case.

## Boundary

This project supports business choices and bounded proposals. It does not promise revenue, market fit or business success, run an autonomous executive team, or replace a CRM, accounting package, ad platform, payment service or market-data provider.

External research gathers evidence; appropriate Production Skills produce domain artefacts; Legal Skills or qualified counsel own legal conclusions. Accounting, tax and regulated financial questions need the appropriate professional. A legal handoff preserves the facts and business intention, asks the specific question, and brings the resulting constraint back into the business decision. Pactwright may govern authorised delivery through an optional integration; it does not own business-model truth or become a core dependency.

## Contributing

Useful contributions identify a concrete business-reasoning defect, missing evidence distinction or reusable mechanism. Keep customer data, credentials and substantial copyrighted source text out of contributions. The repository’s production contribution guide and CI workflow are not yet available.
