## 1. Evidence hierarchy: match support to the claim

This hierarchy is a project decision rule derived from the distinctions in the researched methods, not a universal numerical ranking of sources. A regulator can authoritatively describe its policy without demonstrating commercial efficacy. A randomised trial can estimate a treatment effect without settling legality, business-model transfer or whether the intervention is worth its cost. A mathematical identity can be exact while its input forecast is wrong.

| Claim being evaluated | Preferred support | What that support does not establish | Project treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| What an author or provider says | Original text or first-party documentation, with date, version and reading scope | Truth of the author’s causal claims or a provider’s commercial promises | Preserve the original definition and attribution; distinguish an output-system label from the intended business measure. |
| What occurred in a specific business | Traceable records, direct observations and reconcilable events | Representativeness, causality or performance in a different business | State population, period, provenance, exclusions and known missing records. |
| Why an intervention changed an outcome | A credible identification design with appropriate comparison, uncertainty and exposure checks; replication or synthesis when applicable | Transfer to another population, indefinite persistence or a net-profit guarantee | Inspect design and incentives, not merely publication prestige; retain counter-evidence and uncertainty. |
| Whether a method is plausible | Explicit mechanisms or formal theory, with assumptions and alternatives | Empirical effect size or applicability where the assumptions fail | Use the theory to generate discriminating tests rather than to fabricate customer observations. |
| How professionals organise work | Original professional guidance and relevant operational practice | A universal best workflow or a measured benefit from adopting it | Adapt to the decision and constraints; label the resulting workflow as project application. |
| Whether calculations are correct | Declared definitions, reproducible arithmetic and reconciled inputs | Accurate assumptions, complete costs or viable demand | Separate arithmetic validation, accounting interpretation and scenario uncertainty. |
| Whether a commercial act is allowed | Applicable current jurisdiction-specific law, regulatory guidance and qualified review | Permission in other jurisdictions or for materially different facts | Stage 3 identifies normative support and risk. Stage 9 and qualified reviewers own specific legal analysis. |
| Whether a future commitment is justified | Relevant evidence above plus owner objectives, downside, cost/capacity/cash and decision authority | Certainty that the forecast will occur | Record a bounded decision under uncertainty; do not rename it validated fact. |

The methodological basis includes randomised-versus-observational comparisons [S12](#s12), proportion-test design [S34](#s34), valid sequential inference [S35](#s35), trial replication [S37](#s37) and formal-model limits [S28](#s28), [S42](#s42). Professional and normative sources have different roles, exemplified by [S20](#s20), [S31](#s31) and [S44](#s44).

### Applicability and independence checks

For each material inference, ask what was assigned or observed, which population and outcome were measured, what comparison identified the effect, what uncertainty remains, and which decision would change. Check attrition, selection, differing exposure and the time window when those affect interpretation. Failure to report a design detail in an abstract means it was not assessed here, not that the underlying study passed that check.

A systematic review can improve coverage but still inherits its included studies’ limitations. A precise result from one setting is not automatically more useful than less precise evidence from the actual customer context. Conversely, a local anecdote may expose a broken obligation without establishing its population frequency. Use evidence adequate to the consequence of the decision.

Do not count an original study and a later replication paper that includes it as independent extra studies. Specifically, [S37](#s37) contains the original trial plus three replications, not four new replications. The three Hormozi books are not independent replications of each other; their Stage 2 source attribution remains unchanged. Framework authors and service vendors may share concepts, training traditions and commercial incentives. Independence from the five books means the claim has a separate evidential or professional justification, not merely a different URL repeating the book.

### Research limits and rejected substitutes

The register below is the selected evidence base, not every search hit. Search families covered each of the 27 practice names and targeted the U01–U18 questions, including conflicting findings. Candidate screening favoured original professional sources and primary studies. SEO summaries, unsourced business-book recaps, affiliate reviews and generic claims about universal revenue lifts were not used as proof. Full text was pursued where important and accessible; abstract-only use is identified rather than passed off as full critical appraisal.

Three examples clarify the boundary. The Microsoft full-publication link did not load; [S36](#s36) therefore supports only its abstract-level warning, not an invented reconstruction of its twelve cases. The NBER subscription abstract was readable, but a later fetch failed; [S26](#s26) is not a claim to have audited its full data or model. For [S38](#s38) and [S42](#s42), accessible author-uploaded texts were read after publisher/institutional access limitations; reader comments on those hosting pages were not treated as evidence.

Strategyzer’s older 2015 article, *Roadmap to test your value proposition* (https://www.strategyzer.com/library/roadmap-to-test-your-value-proposition), was also inspected. It separates customer needs, offer features and payment tests, but proposes a “fake-sales” website. This stage does not silently relabel that proposal as an honest presale, nor adopt deceptive testing. The current creator explanation [S05](#s05) supports representing and testing fit; the bootstrap’s truthfulness requirement independently limits how any test may be performed. This is a rejected tactic candidate, not a rejected need for testing.

No independent validation of the complete $100M playbooks or a universal Value Equation was established by this bounded search. That is an access/search conclusion, not proof that no such evidence exists anywhere. The [unresolved-claims record](2026-09-10-stage-03-claims-failures-and-principles.md#unresolved-claims) states what remains unproved and the evidence that could change the decision. These are research uncertainties expressly required by Stage 3, not missing user-owned decisions or a waiver of the stage.

## 2. Source register

Each selected source below was actually inspected at the stated scope. Live pages were accessed for this stage on **10 September 2026**; older studies retain their publication dates. Search-result text from an original publisher or author was sometimes the accessible abstract. No claim of full-text review is made for those entries. A bibliographic URL points to the source, not a promise of perpetual public access. PDFs, full articles, screenshots, raw extracts and proprietary examples are not repository outputs.

### S07: Jonas Schmidt and Tammo H. A. Bijmolt, Accurately measuring willingness to pay for consumer goods: a meta-analysis of the hypothetical bias

**Publication/access:** 2020; Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science 48, 499–518; DOI 10.1007/s11747-019-00666-6.  
**Source:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11747-019-00666-6  
**Evidence class:** Empirical meta-analysis.

**Material inspected:** Read the article’s methods, pooled results and moderators: 77 studies in 47 papers, 115 effect sizes.

## 4. Decisions and downstream use

Retain the 22 capability responsibilities as a provisional organising map, not a selected skill count. Replace ambiguous metric shorthand with explicit meanings at the point of use. Preserve branded terminology when identifying a source, but avoid requiring a branded playbook to perform the business task. Do not replace source definitions silently: document the mapping, incompatibility or project choice.

The next-stage business-system model should reference only the evidence and distinctions it needs. This record does not select an execution provider, create an accounting package, impose a universal ontology or establish jurisdiction-specific legal rules. Those boundaries remain as accepted in Stage 1.
