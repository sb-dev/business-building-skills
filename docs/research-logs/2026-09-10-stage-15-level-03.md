### Expected artefacts

A complete linked business dossier, transparent workload/economics scenarios, a minimal acquisition/sales brief and one decision-changing experiment. Do not create unrelated outputs or replace source evidence with the assessment. A blocked commitment can still have a complete, useful design or review record.

### Discriminating acceptance

Each visit uses 1.5 hours and 25 cash cost. The 24-visit plan uses 44 total owner hours, has 2160 receipts, 600 variable cash cost, 1400 after overhead available before tax/owner draws, and 520 after valuing all 44 owner hours at 20. Forty visit-hours allow at most 26 whole visits, not 32; at 26 visits cash after overhead is 1530, below the 1800 objective. This bound does not prove a route or sufficient demand. The verifier checks the fixed arithmetic; direct review must also examine evidence scope, causal limits and the requested business reasoning. A favourable calculation alone does not pass all growth gates.

### Negative control

Fail a 32-visit capacity claim based on service minutes alone, counting the same owner labour as both imputed and extra cash expense, or claiming six trials establish monthly repeat demand.

### Preservation and smallest responsible repair

Keep basic-bicycle-service-v1 and one-neighbourhood-v1. Show any proposed new price separately from the accepted 90; do not mutate bookings or safety/competence boundaries.
