# Business Building Skills — System Specification

**Version:** 1.0 · **Status:** Specified, not implemented · **Date:** 9 September 2026

## Mission

Turn uncertain business opportunities and existing-business problems into evidence-backed commercial decisions across customer, value, offer, monetisation, acquisition, sales, delivery, continued value, economics, cash, experiments and constraint repair.

The initial five books are evidence inputs, not the architecture. Their accessible concepts were reconciled in Stage 2 and independently challenged in Stage 3. Full-book access gaps remain documented; no skill may claim unseen source material.

## Scope and boundaries

Own business-system judgement, working business artefacts and learning loops. Reuse specialist systems for CRM, messaging, ads, analytics, billing, accounting, surveys and scheduling. Legal/tax/regulated conclusions remain specialist handoffs. Core remains useful without Pactwright or sibling Production Skills.

## Governing principles

```text
evidence before scale
customer reality before offer optimisation
truthfulness before conversion
business system before isolated tactic
cheapest adequate experiment
facts / assumptions / synthetic fixtures remain distinct
revenue ≠ contribution ≠ cash
lead quality and downstream value matter
retention changes acquisition economics
cash and capacity can block growth
diagnose before redesign
preserve supported decisions
smallest sufficient correction
```

No universal business score, growth target, pricing formula, CRM, analytics runtime or executive-agent hierarchy.

## Architecture

```text
business-build
→ opportunity / customer / value / offer / price / delivery / economics / experiment

business-grow
→ channel / acquisition / sales / conversion / retention / expansion / bounded scale

business-evaluate
→ evidence / offer / economics / growth / experiment / claims / constraint / repair

business-pack-author
→ research / specialise / showcase / differential eval / validate

optional Extension Pack
→ business-model defaults and constraints

external/deterministic execution
→ calculations, CRM, messaging, ads, analytics, billing, accounting, collection
```

## Core skills

### `business-build`

Commands: `frame-opportunity`, `model-customer-value`, `design-offer`, `design-pricing-monetisation`, `model-delivery-economics`, `design-business-experiment`, `build-business-brief`.

### `business-grow`

Commands: `select-channel`, `design-acquisition-test`, `design-sales-path`, `diagnose-conversion`, `design-retention-expansion`, `scale-channel`.

### `business-evaluate`

Commands: `audit-evidence`, `evaluate-offer-economics`, `evaluate-acquisition-sales`, `evaluate-retention-cash`, `evaluate-experiment`, `audit-claims`, `diagnose-constraint`, `recommend-smallest-change`.

### `business-pack-author`

Commands: `inspect-catalogue`, `research-business-model`, `define-specialisation`, `define-core-effects`, `build-pack-showcase`, `build-pack-evals`, `compare-core-vs-pack`, `validate-pack`.

## Execution boundary

Generative skills own framing and judgement. Deterministic scripts/spreadsheets own arithmetic/validation. External systems own their state and side effects. Provider adapters are optional. Tool credentials are not authority; external writes require applicable approval.

## Professional and ethical boundary

Unsupported claims, fake scarcity/testimonials, hidden material pricing/renewal and unauthorised marketing activation fail before optimisation. Business Building identifies issue, preserves facts, blocks affected activation and prepares a scoped handoff. It does not manufacture legal certainty.

## Build order

1. deterministic economics/validation utilities;
2. `business-build` first vertical;
3. `business-evaluate` first vertical and preservation repair;
4. professional-service fixture;
5. `business-grow`;
6. `business-pack-author` + `professional-services` pack differential;
7. progressive examples and canonical stress-test coverage;
8. optional provider integrations only where demonstrated.

## Acceptance

`working` requires a real agent execution of the first vertical with deterministic arithmetic and critical adversarial/preservation tests. `benchmarked` additionally requires command coverage, three stress-test families, 15 primary prompts, one pack differential and clean external installation. Documentation alone cannot establish either.
