# Business Building Skills — System Specification

**Status:** Canonical architecture specification  
**Derived from:** accepted bootstrap research Stages 1–17 on `feat/bootstrap-2`  
**Stage 18 authority:** bootstrap §25  
**Boundary:** this document specifies the target system. It does not claim the skills, packs, installation path or benchmark suite are already implemented.

## 1. Mission

Business Building Skills provides reusable business-building judgement for turning uncertain customer, value and economic assumptions into coherent business choices, testing the riskiest assumptions with adequate evidence, building truthful offers and acquisition systems, checking delivery/economics/cash before commitment, and repairing the current constraint without unnecessary redesign.

The system connects:

```text
customer / problem
→ value
→ offer
→ pricing / monetisation
→ acquisition
→ sales / conversion
→ delivery
→ retention / expansion / referral
→ unit economics / cash
→ assumptions / experiments / evidence
→ constraint diagnosis
→ smallest sufficient correction
```

It is a **Production Skills family** project. It owns reusable business-building production intelligence and business-decision artefacts. It does not own every system or professional discipline needed to operate a business.

## 2. Scope and professional boundary

### 2.1 Owned outcomes

The system owns bounded reasoning and artefacts for:

1. opportunity framing;
2. customer/problem evidence;
3. value and alternatives;
4. offer construction and obligations;
5. pricing and packaging;
6. revenue/money-model design;
7. channel fit and bounded acquisition growth;
8. engagement, qualification and sales-path design;
9. truthful commercial communication requirements;
10. delivery, capacity and quality reasoning;
11. retention, repeat, renewal, expansion and referral reasoning;
12. unit economics, metric meaning and cash exposure;
13. assumption identification and experiment design;
14. measurement and learning interpretation;
15. constraint diagnosis and preservation-aware repair;
16. business-system review and specialist handoffs;
17. reusable business-model specialisation through Extension Packs.

These responsibilities consolidate the accepted C01–C22 capability taxonomy. They are responsibilities, not a requirement for one skill per capability.

### 2.2 Work explicitly outside the core

Business Building Skills does **not** replace:

- market/deep research execution;
- legal advice, tax advice, accounting treatment, investment advice or regulated professional judgement;
- product/software implementation, UI/UX, brand/creative/media production;
- CRM state, campaign sending, ad buying, billing, accounting, analytics warehousing, survey collection or A/B assignment;
- payment processing, inventory systems, scheduling systems or customer databases;
- Pactwright lifecycle governance;
- the five source books or any other source corpus.

A business recommendation is not authority to publish, send, spend, charge, migrate data, hire, sign, borrow or change a live customer obligation.

### 2.3 Intended users and roles

The target users are accountable business owners/leads, functional contributors, consultants/advisers and AI agents assisting them. The system also collaborates with specialist reviewers and execution operators. These roles remain distinct:

| Role | Primary responsibility | Authority boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Accountable owner | accepts business decisions and commitments | business risk and action approval remain human/accountable-owner responsibilities |
| Functional contributor | supplies evidence and proposes bounded changes | cross-functional commitments require the appropriate owner |
| Consultant/adviser | structures analysis and recommendations | cannot bind the client or claim unperformed specialist review |
| AI agent | organises facts, assumptions, artefacts and evaluations | cannot manufacture observations, professional authority or external-action permission |
| Specialist reviewer | returns research/legal/accounting/etc. findings within remit | findings stay attributed and scoped |
| Execution operator/system | performs approved external effects and returns evidence | tool access is not business authority |

## 3. Source-book and evidence relationship

The initial evidence corpus consists of:

1. *$100M Offers*;
2. *$100M Leads*;
3. *$100M Money Models*;
4. *The Personal MBA*;
5. *The Lean Startup*.

The repository encodes **capabilities, not books**. The canonical transformation is:

```text
source idea
→ independently expressed business capability
→ production responsibility
→ command / workflow
→ evaluation criterion
→ benchmark case
```

Rules:

- source agreement is evidence for investigating a capability, not proof of universal effectiveness;
- same-author overlap is not independent replication;
- book examples and branded terminology are not repository structure;
- conflicting recommendations are retained and qualified by context and broader professional/empirical research;
- substantial copyrighted passages, proprietary examples and chapter structures are not reproduced;
- source facts, customer observations, synthetic fixtures and project synthesis remain distinguishable.

## 4. Governing principles

These principles are mandatory system invariants.

### P01 — Evidence before scale
Do not scale acquisition, hiring, inventory, infrastructure or paid media while critical assumptions, delivery feasibility or economics remain materially unresolved.

### P02 — Customer reality before offer optimisation
Do not optimise bonuses, copy, guarantees or urgency around a weakly evidenced customer problem.

### P03 — Truthfulness is non-compensatory
False scarcity, fabricated urgency, invented testimonials, unsupported guarantees/ROI, hidden material terms and obstructive customer journeys fail regardless of conversion benefit.

### P04 — Business model before isolated tactic
Assess a tactic inside the customer → value → acquisition → conversion → delivery → economics → retention system.

### P05 — Cheapest **adequate** experiment
Minimise effort only after choosing evidence capable of changing the decision. A cheaper invalid proxy is not preferred.

### P06 — Assumption, observation, metric, interpretation and decision remain separate
Generated plausibility is never upgraded to observation. Missing evidence is not zero.

### P07 — Revenue is not economics
Revenue, contribution, wider sustainability, cash, capacity and customer obligations remain distinct.

### P08 — Diagnose the binding constraint
Do not optimise every metric simultaneously or equate the lowest percentage with causality.

### P09 — Acquisition quality outranks raw lead volume
Judge acquisition using fit, qualification, buyer progression, full acquisition effort and downstream value/economics.

### P10 — Retention changes acquisition economics
Where continued value matters, acquisition cannot be assessed independently of retention/repeat/renewal, refunds, service burden and cohort maturity.

### P11 — Cash timing is first-class
A profitable or contribution-positive model can still be unfundable. Dated usable cash can block scale.

### P12 — Preserve validated decisions
An accepted customer, offer, price, channel or delivery choice stays protected unless evidence implicates it.

### P13 — Correct the smallest responsible layer
Prefer no change, containment, local repair or bounded test before coordinated redesign. Broader change requires evidence of coupled responsibility.

### P14 — Business quality is multidimensional
Never collapse customer evidence, truthfulness, value, economics, cash, capacity, experiment quality and other dimensions into one universal score.

### P15 — Legal and ethical constraints are production inputs
Identify the issue, preserve facts, obtain the appropriate specialist constraint, then adapt the business proposal. Business Building does not author legal conclusions.

### P16 — External execution is explicit and separate
Tool availability, credentials, a successful calculation or a good assessment do not confer permission to act.

## 5. Business-system architecture

### 5.1 Authoritative business dossier

The smallest useful representation is an ordinary versioned **business dossier**, not a universal ontology or graph service. A dossier can be one document for a small case and may link to separately owned evidence/calculation/specialist records as the case grows.

The business map contains fifteen concerns:

| ID | Concern |
|---|---|
| B01 | target customer |
| B02 | problem / desired progress |
| B03 | alternative / status quo |
| B04 | value proposition |
| B05 | offer |
| B06 | acquisition channel |
| B07 | sales / conversion path |
| B08 | price / revenue mechanism |
| B09 | delivery mechanism |
| B10 | cost structure |
| B11 | retention / repeat / expansion |
| B12 | referral / advocacy |
| B13 | operational constraints |
| B14 | cash timing |
| B15 | key assumptions |

Each material claim is one of: **known observation**, **hypothesis**, **mixed evidence**, **unknown**, or **not applicable with reason**. These are evidence states, not approval states.

### 5.2 Customer and value model

Distinguish the declared target customer, observed customer, buyer, user, economic decision-maker and influencer where material; add payer/gatekeeper roles only when they change the decision. Do not manufacture personas to fill a template.

Value is represented relative to an actual alternative and can use relevant dimensions such as functional, economic, emotional, social/status, risk, time, effort and uncertainty reduction. No offer must contain every dimension and no composite value score is required.

### 5.3 Business control loop

The canonical control loop is:

```text
bounded objective / decision
→ inspect accepted versions and evidence
→ expose material assumptions and constraints
→ construct or assess the responsible business component
→ request deterministic calculation / research / specialist or operational evidence where needed
→ evaluate evidence and independent gates
→ decide retain / revise / test / reject / block / no change
→ if change: name responsible layer + protected decisions + authority
→ execute only through authorised owner/system
→ capture returned evidence
→ record learning
→ re-diagnose the current constraint
```

The loop is deliberately document-led and provider-independent.

## 6. Capability and skill architecture

### 6.1 Native responsibility groups

The accepted native residuals are:

- **NC01** evidence-qualified opportunity, customer and value framing;
- **NC02** coherent offer, pricing and money-model reasoning;
- **NC03** qualified acquisition, sales and retained-growth reasoning;
- **NC04** delivery, economics and cash commitment review;
- **NC05** assumption, experiment and learning discipline;
- **NC06** constraint diagnosis and preservation-aware repair;
- **NC07** truthfulness and cross-domain constraint handoffs;
- **NC08** business-model specialisation qualification and authoring.

Existing research, collection, operational, calculation and specialist systems remain reused rather than reimplemented.

### 6.2 Four core skills

| Skill | Owns | Default mutation mode |
|---|---|---|
| `business-build` | construct/revise bounded business choices, economics, assumptions and experiments | requested business artefacts only |
| `business-grow` | design qualified acquisition, sales, retention, referral, expansion and bounded scale | proposals/briefs only; no live campaigns |
| `business-evaluate` | read-only assessment, diagnosis and smallest-change recommendation | separate assessment; never silently edits subject |
| `business-pack-author` | research, define, demonstrate and validate reusable business-model specialisations | pack-design/evaluation artefacts only |

There are 32 logical command contracts: 10 build, 8 grow, 7 evaluate, 7 pack-author. A command is a logical task selector, not a required shell/slash runtime. See [Repository and Contracts](03-business-building-skills-repository-and-contracts-spec.md).

### 6.3 Composition

Composition is through explicit artefact/evidence references. No skill requires another sibling installation to enforce its own invariants. A skill must remain useful when installed selectively with its own required references/templates/scripts.

Extension Packs are optional specialisations of these command contracts; they do not replace core skills or create broader mutation permissions. See [Customisation Packs](05-business-building-customisation-packs-spec.md).

## 7. Execution boundary

The selected execution model is **document-led reasoning plus task-scoped existing execution systems/operators**.

Business Building owns:

- business framing and customer/problem reasoning;
- offer, pricing and monetisation reasoning;
- channel and sales-path design;
- experiment design and metric interpretation;
- constraint diagnosis, economics interpretation and smallest correction.

Existing systems/operators own arithmetic, spreadsheets, CRM state, email sending, ad execution, analytics, billing, accounting, survey collection, A/B assignment, warehousing, hosting and other operational effects.

Every external handoff retains:

1. decision and protected baseline;
2. requested evidence/action;
3. source and semantic mapping;
4. actual task-specific tool/operator binding;
5. authority and constraints;
6. actual result and reconciliation;
7. failure/retry/idempotency conditions;
8. learning/change impact.

Provider substitution is permitted only when the replacement preserves relevant semantic identity, units, windows, error semantics, authority and data boundaries. A provider change must not redesign the business workflow merely because schemas differ.

## 8. Legal and ethical boundary

### 8.1 Three independent findings

For a legally/ethically sensitive action, keep three findings distinct:

1. **project truthfulness/treatment boundary** — can reject known deception without issuing a legal opinion;
2. **applicable specialist constraint** — supplied by Legal Skills/counsel/qualified reviewer within remit;
3. **action authority** — supplied by the accountable owner/operator for the exact effect.

None substitutes for another.

### 8.2 Seven-step handoff

```text
identify issue
→ preserve facts
→ state business intention
→ surface assumptions
→ route legal/professional question
→ consume returned constraint
→ adjust offer / channel / process
```

Relevant topics include advertising substantiation, comparisons, guarantees, scarcity/urgency, endorsements, direct marketing, privacy/tracking, subscription/renewal/cancellation/refunds, pricing, credit, financial promotions/earnings claims, vulnerable users, competition, employment/contractor matters, tax and regulated sectors.

A material unresolved specialist question blocks the affected commitment. It does not automatically block unrelated safe analysis.

## 9. Implementation build order

This is the target implementation dependency order, not a maturity claim:

1. **Canonical contracts** — preserve these six specs and accepted command/artefact definitions.
2. **Self-contained core skill shells** — implement the four skill entry points and local references without a compulsory common runtime.
3. **Core vertical** — prove one bounded assumption → evidence → evaluation → repair workflow before broad growth automation.
4. **Deterministic/reference checks** — implement arithmetic/schema/prompt/link/fixture checks and retained regressions.
5. **Progressive examples** — expose the accepted 15 prompts and stable public example paths; add real outputs only when executed.
6. **Canonical stress tests** — run the three structures against the actual installed product and retain results.
7. **Representative Extension Packs** — implement only qualified pack candidates, executing core-vs-pack comparisons rather than assuming benefit.
8. **Clean installation and selective-install validation** — prove self-containment outside the source checkout.
9. **Optional integrations** — add only justified provider/Pactwright bindings after core portability is proven.
10. **Release/maturity gates** — publish claims only when actual implementation/evaluation evidence supports them.

Do not begin by building a CRM, analytics warehouse, accounting engine, ad platform, universal business graph or autonomous executive team.

## 10. System acceptance

A conforming implementation must demonstrate, rather than merely describe:

- source ideas remain traceable while skills stay capability-shaped;
- customer/problem/value claims preserve evidence versus assumptions;
- offers expose complete obligations, proof, delivery and economic consequences;
- acquisition uses downstream quality rather than raw volume alone;
- delivery/capacity, contribution and dated cash can independently block scale;
- experiment rules are falsifiable and frozen before interpretation;
- constraint diagnosis considers rival explanations and preserves valid decisions;
- deceptive growth shortcuts fail even if commercially attractive;
- external actions require their own authority and return evidence;
- core works without Extension Packs;
- implemented packs obey precedence and materially specialise behaviour;
- the 15 progressive examples, three canonical stress tests and benchmark architecture remain reproducible;
- clean selective installation proves skill self-containment;
- no public maturity or benchmark claim exceeds actual evidence.

Testing contracts and release gates are defined in [Testing and Benchmark](04-testing-and-benchmark-spec.md).
