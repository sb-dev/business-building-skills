### PK01 — `saas-business`

**Design disposition:** RETAIN AS DESIGN CANDIDATE. **Implementation status:** NOT IMPLEMENTED.

**Research basis:** R01 in the research companion.

**Fit:** Recurring access to a hosted software capability, including seat, usage or hybrid charging. Establish the actual service and commercial unit; a downloadable file, an agency calling itself SaaS or a subscription label alone is insufficient.

**Non-activation:** Do not apply to an exclusively one-time digital download or a human-delivered engagement without hosted-service economics. Store-mediated consumer subscription questions are assessed separately in PK02.

**Core/catalogue comparison:** Core already models subscriptions, value, costs and cash. The residual is a reusable account/user/meter/contract bridge and service-cost sensitivity, not another billing system. No subscription price, margin or churn threshold is inferred from R01.

| Effect | Affected commands | Required changed behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| PK01-E1 | B02, G04 | Separate user, account, payer and economic approver; request adoption and purchasing evidence at the appropriate unit; return a role-to-value and buying-path map. |
| PK01-E2 | B04, B07, E03 | Request consumed, billable and invoiced units with price/cost versions; return a metering/consideration bridge and cohort contribution, including unbilled use and support burden. |
| PK01-E3 | G06, G08, E02 | Request meaningful-use, renewal, expansion and service-quality evidence on matched horizons; assess marginal growth without replacing delivery or cash gates with MRR. |

All effects preserve the shared evidence, rights, calculation, mutation and growth invariants; they add no external authority.

| Dimension | Original dimension | Candidate treatment |
|---|---|---|
| D01 | customer / buyer structure | Account, user, payer and budget authority may differ; preserve their evidence. |
| D02 | offer grammar | Bounded service access, limits, support and change obligations; no mandatory tier count. |
| D03 | pricing unit | Seat, account, usage or justified hybrid; distinguish value from the billing unit. |
| D04 | money model | Recurring, usage and services components reconciled without double-counting consideration. |
| D05 | channel priorities | Compare product-led, content, partner or sales-led fit using qualified retained outcomes. |
| D06 | sales path | Connect evaluation, adoption and buying approval; free activation is not paid conversion. |
| D07 | delivery model | Hosted operation, onboarding, integration and support dependencies remain operator-owned. |
| D08 | retention model | Account and revenue cohorts, voluntary renewal, usage and expansion stay distinct. |
| D09 | unit economics | Include marginal infrastructure/usage and support; expose cost changes at higher consumption. |
| D10 | capacity constraints | Technical limits and human onboarding/support capacity both constrain commitments. |
| D11 | cash behaviour | Prepaid obligations, arrears billing, collection and refunds enter the dated cash view. |
| D12 | core metrics | Defined account retention, contribution per cohort and consumed-to-billed unit reconciliation. |
| D13 | experiment types | Bounded onboarding, price comprehension or usage-model test tied to a decision. |
| D14 | quality criteria | Value actually delivered, reliability and intelligible limits; no billing success as value proof. |

**Discriminating design case S01:** Compare aggregate positive contribution with a loss-making high-usage account. The required result retains both facts, checks unit mismatch and does not declare safe scale while other gates are unknown. Exact inputs/prompts and the actual bounded local comparisons are in the [design probes](research-logs/2026-09-10-stage-14-design-probes.md). They do not establish installed-pack performance.

### PK02 — `consumer-mobile-subscription`

**Design disposition:** RETAIN AS DESIGN CANDIDATE. **Implementation status:** NOT IMPLEMENTED.

**Research basis:** R02, R03 in the research companion.

**Fit:** Consumer recurring or prepaid access delivered through a mobile application where store-managed plans, payment or entitlement behaviour materially affects the business decision. Verify actual purchase channel and applicable configuration.

**Non-activation:** A mobile interface to a direct-billed service with no relevant store mechanics does not automatically need this grammar. A prepaid arrangement must not be treated as auto-renewing merely because it is listed under subscriptions.

**Core/catalogue comparison:** PK01 covers generic recurring software, but store offers, restored access, grace/recovery and channel-specific cash need an explicit consumer entitlement model. Retain independently useful mobile detail without importing platform SDKs or universal commission rates.

| Effect | Affected commands | Required changed behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| PK02-E1 | B03, B04, E05 | Request actual offer, billing/commitment period, post-offer price and entitlement terms by channel; return a truthful transition map, not an invented free-trial or renewal default. |
| PK02-E2 | G06, E02 | Reconcile paid renewals, entitlement continuation, grace/recovery and meaningful use by due cohort; do not count an entitled or active user as a settled renewal. |
| PK02-E3 | B07, E03, G08 | Request gross customer payment, adjustments, platform charges, net proceeds and settlement dates; check remaining service/refund obligations before paid acquisition. |

All effects preserve the shared evidence, rights, calculation, mutation and growth invariants; they add no external authority.

| Dimension | Original dimension | Candidate treatment |
|---|---|---|
| D01 | customer / buyer structure | User, purchaser and platform-mediated payer/entitlement roles are recorded where different. |
| D02 | offer grammar | Access, introductory terms, renewal transitions, restoration and cancellation responsibilities. |
| D03 | pricing unit | Plan/duration/offer or prepaid unit; commitment period and charge frequency remain separate. |
| D04 | money model | Own subscription consideration and store proceeds reconcile; no universal fee assumption. |
| D05 | channel priorities | Compare store discovery, direct reach and other channels on retained net economics. |
| D06 | sales path | Install, trial, purchase, entitlement and renewal are different events with specific owners. |
| D07 | delivery model | App access and support depend on validated entitlement and product delivery, not receipt alone. |
| D08 | retention model | Renewal due, successful payment, grace, voluntary exit and active use remain distinct. |
| D09 | unit economics | Channel/cohort costs, refunds, support and service usage accompany acquisition cost. |
| D10 | capacity constraints | Infrastructure and human support/recovery work remain finite; store status is not capacity. |
| D11 | cash behaviour | Settlement delays, withholding and remaining access/refund promises are dated explicitly. |
| D12 | core metrics | Due-cohort paid-renewal rate, entitlement coverage and meaningful-use rate are separate. |
| D13 | experiment types | Honest offer comprehension, onboarding or bounded pricing test with approved exposure. |
| D14 | quality criteria | Clear material terms, valid access recovery and legitimate exit; no cancellation obstruction. |

**Discriminating design case S02:** A cohort with 90 paid renewals, 95 continuing entitlements and 60 active users must not become 95 satisfied paying renewals. No platform rule substitutes for the actual applicable legal finding. Exact inputs/prompts and the actual bounded local comparisons are in the [design probes](research-logs/2026-09-10-stage-14-design-probes.md). They do not establish installed-pack performance.

### PK03 — `professional-services`

**Design disposition:** RETAIN AS DESIGN CANDIDATE. **Implementation status:** NOT IMPLEMENTED.

**Research basis:** R04, R05, R06, R07 in the research companion.

**Fit:** Delivery depends materially on bounded human expertise, time, quality and client participation. Fixed-fee, time-based, milestone and retainer structures are alternatives, not prescribed models.

**Non-activation:** Do not apply solely because founders are professionals. A scalable self-serve product without material engagement delivery is better served by its product mechanism. Regulated advice retains the specialist boundary.

**Core/catalogue comparison:** Core already checks resources and costs. This grammar adds engagement scope, acceptance, client dependency and sales/non-billable time consistently. Consulting fits the explicit advisory mode described in PK04; no required second package.

| Effect | Affected commands | Required changed behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| PK03-E1 | B03, B06, G04 | Request deliverables, exclusions, change control, client inputs and acceptance owner; return an engagement scope and dependency map with realistic obligations. |
| PK03-E2 | B04, B07, E03 | Compare time, fixed fee, milestones and bounded retainers against priced and unpriced effort; separate contracted, delivered, accepted, invoiced and collected amounts. |
| PK03-E3 | G08, E06, E07 | Include sales, administration, support and client-delay exposure in shared capacity; bound intake or repair the implicated scope instead of inventing staff or changing the whole business. |

All effects preserve the shared evidence, rights, calculation, mutation and growth invariants; they add no external authority.

| Dimension | Original dimension | Candidate treatment |
|---|---|---|
| D01 | customer / buyer structure | Sponsor, user, procurement/budget and acceptance authority may be different client roles. |
| D02 | offer grammar | Deliverables, exclusions, dependencies, acceptance and change control; advisory mode adds transfer. |
| D03 | pricing unit | Engagement, milestone, time or bounded retainer; outcome-linked fees need reviewed conditions. |
| D04 | money model | Project and ongoing support exchanges are explicit; a retainer does not mean unlimited work. |
| D05 | channel priorities | Network, referrals, expertise content and lawful outreach tested against actual buyer fit. |
| D06 | sales path | Qualification, discovery, proposal and client acceptance require actual buyer actions. |
| D07 | delivery model | Human work, client input, review, handover and rework form the delivery model. |
| D08 | retention model | Repeat project, retained scope and expansion need new value and available capacity. |
| D09 | unit economics | Include sales/non-billable effort and rework; distinguish cash cost from valued owner time. |
| D10 | capacity constraints | Resource-specific hours and dates include shared work and current obligations. |
| D11 | cash behaviour | Deposits, milestone acceptance, invoicing and collection dates can diverge. |
| D12 | core metrics | Scoped effective rate, contribution, utilisation basis, acceptance delays and unpaid work. |
| D13 | experiment types | Bounded paid diagnostic or delivery test; no guaranteed client ROI inferred from interest. |
| D14 | quality criteria | Acceptance evidence, competence, scope honesty, client confidentiality and transfer quality. |

**Discriminating design case S03:** Profitable engagements can exceed shared human capacity. A retainer or fixed fee never makes capacity unlimited or collections certain. Preserve approved client scope while proposing a bounded intake change. Exact inputs/prompts and the actual bounded local comparisons are in the [design probes](research-logs/2026-09-10-stage-14-design-probes.md). They do not establish installed-pack performance.

### PK04 — `consulting-business`

**Design disposition:** MERGE INTO PROFESSIONAL-SERVICES ADVISORY MODE. **Implementation status:** NOT IMPLEMENTED.

**Research basis:** R04, R05, R06, R07 in the research companion.

**Fit:** Advice, diagnosis or knowledge transfer is the bounded engagement outcome, and client action materially mediates realised impact. Select the advisory mode only when these facts are relevant.

**Non-activation:** A consultant title alone does not activate the mode. Standard execution work can use PK03 without extra advisory gates. Neither this name nor a client signature grants regulated-advice authority.

**Core/catalogue comparison:** The additional responsibilities are client problem framing, sponsorship, adopted recommendations and knowledge transfer. They fit PK03 inputs and acceptance fields without incompatible pricing/cash assumptions. Retain the seed and distinct checks as a mode, not a duplicated independently installed pack or a hidden parent dependency.

| Effect | Affected commands | Required changed behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| PK04-E1 | B03, G04 | Separate diagnostic question, sponsor expectations, recommendation deliverable and client decision; define what the engagement can promise versus what depends on client implementation. |
| PK04-E2 | B06, E02 | Request knowledge-transfer and client acceptance evidence; distinguish a delivered report, accepted advice, implemented action and achieved benefit. |
| PK04-E3 | B09, E04, E07 | Test whether the advice can change the decision and whether the client can act; preserve inconclusive impact rather than inventing attributed ROI or escalating into broad transformation. |

All effects preserve the shared evidence, rights, calculation, mutation and growth invariants; they add no external authority.

| Dimension | Original dimension | Candidate treatment |
|---|---|---|
| D01 | customer / buyer structure | Add sponsor, decision recipient and implementation owner to inherited service roles. |
| D02 | offer grammar | Add bounded diagnosis, recommendations and knowledge/skills transfer; do not promise client adoption. |
| D03 | pricing unit | Inherit PK03 options; outcome-fee conditions must distinguish controllable from client-dependent work. |
| D04 | money model | Inherit PK03 exchange model; no extra revenue line merely because advice is strategic. |
| D05 | channel priorities | Emphasise evidence of relevant expertise and trusted introductions only when audience fit supports it. |
| D06 | sales path | Add problem sponsorship and decision/implementation readiness to service qualification. |
| D07 | delivery model | Add discovery access, evidence review, recommendation acceptance and transfer responsibilities. |
| D08 | retention model | Follow-on work requires a new client need; dependency creation is not a retention mechanism. |
| D09 | unit economics | Inherit service costing, adding discovery, review and transfer effort rather than ignoring it. |
| D10 | capacity constraints | Inherit human resource constraints; client review and stakeholder access also bound feasibility. |
| D11 | cash behaviour | Inherit dated collections; acceptance-dependent milestones remain conditional until evidenced. |
| D12 | core metrics | Separate recommendation delivery, acceptance, adoption and attributed impact; no assumed ROI. |
| D13 | experiment types | Bounded diagnostic or decision workshop with stated possible outcomes and client dependencies. |
| D14 | quality criteria | Competence, independence, confidentiality and usable transfer, without claiming professional certification. |

**Discriminating design case S04:** A delivered report does not establish client acceptance, adoption or economic improvement. The advisory mode may require more evidence while preserving the same service arithmetic and exact client commitments. Exact inputs/prompts and the actual bounded local comparisons are in the [design probes](research-logs/2026-09-10-stage-14-design-probes.md). They do not establish installed-pack performance.

### PK05 — `ecommerce-business`

**Design disposition:** RETAIN AS DESIGN CANDIDATE. **Implementation status:** NOT IMPLEMENTED.

**Research basis:** R08 in the research companion.

**Fit:** The business sells physical goods with material stock, fulfilment, returns or supplier commitments. Establish ownership, fulfilment arrangement and actual stock states, including outsourced delivery.

**Non-activation:** An exclusively digital download needs no invented physical stock. Intermediary matching with third-party sellers is PK06 unless an explicitly separate owned-stock component exists.

**Core/catalogue comparison:** Core includes costs and cash; the reusable residual is a SKU/order/stock-state bridge, fulfilment and reverse-flow exposure. Provider inventory grades cannot replace contribution, available stock or funding checks.

| Effect | Affected commands | Required changed behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| PK05-E1 | B03, B06 | Request order promises, available/committed/incoming stock, supplier lead times and fulfilment/return responsibilities; return a feasible goods-flow and obligation model. |
| PK05-E2 | B07, E03 | Reconcile own consideration, discounts/refunds, goods consumed, shipping, returns and stock purchases; separate inventory valuation/expense from cash without double-counting. |
| PK05-E3 | G01, G08, E06 | Assess acquisition against inventory and working-capital constraints; use demand and dated obligations before proposing replenishment, not a revenue grade or universal stock target. |

All effects preserve the shared evidence, rights, calculation, mutation and growth invariants; they add no external authority.

| Dimension | Original dimension | Candidate treatment |
|---|---|---|
| D01 | customer / buyer structure | Customer, recipient, stock owner, supplier and fulfilment partner have distinct roles. |
| D02 | offer grammar | Goods, delivery timing, condition, exclusions and legitimate return/refund promises. |
| D03 | pricing unit | Unit, bundle or justified recurring shipment; packing and fulfilment consequences explicit. |
| D04 | money model | Own goods revenue and complementary services reconciled separately from third-party funds. |
| D05 | channel priorities | Channel demand judged by fulfilled contribution and return quality, not click volume. |
| D06 | sales path | Order, payment, fulfilment, delivery and return are separate commercial states. |
| D07 | delivery model | SKU-level stock, pick/pack/ship and reverse flow with actual supplier dependencies. |
| D08 | retention model | Repeat demand and voluntary replenishment are observed, not assumed for every item. |
| D09 | unit economics | Goods cost, logistics, payment, acquisition, damage and return handling on named bases. |
| D10 | capacity constraints | Available stock and fulfilment resources constrain promises; incoming is not always available. |
| D11 | cash behaviour | Stock outlay, supplier terms, settlement and refund dates govern growth pace. |
| D12 | core metrics | Stock reconciliation, defined sell-through, return rate and contribution per fulfilled cohort. |
| D13 | experiment types | Bounded assortment, honest preorder or fulfilment test with funding and delivery safeguards. |
| D14 | quality criteria | Deliverable promise, accurate product claims, safe goods and intact customer entitlements. |

**Discriminating design case S05:** A positive contribution and ending cash can conceal a pre-collection stock-funding gap. Reject a replenishment claim based only on revenue rank, and keep returns separate from stock adjustments. Exact inputs/prompts and the actual bounded local comparisons are in the [design probes](research-logs/2026-09-10-stage-14-design-probes.md). They do not establish installed-pack performance.

### PK06 — `marketplace-business`

**Design disposition:** RETAIN AS DESIGN CANDIDATE. **Implementation status:** NOT IMPLEMENTED.

**Research basis:** R09, R10 in the research companion.

**Fit:** The business intermediates distinct sides whose matching or transaction progress and incentives materially determine value. Establish the actual roles, transaction/funds flows and the relevant local or category market.

**Non-activation:** A direct seller with its own stock is not a marketplace merely because its website lists many products. Interest from suppliers alone is not buyer demand or demonstrated matching liquidity.

**Core/catalogue comparison:** Core models roles and economics; the reusable residual is side-specific outcomes and constrained matching with own-fee/funds reconciliation. R09 supplies a useful two-sided distinction but its inverted rate wording is explicitly rejected.

| Effect | Affected commands | Required changed behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| PK06-E1 | B02, G01, G04 | Request each side’s progress, alternatives, eligibility and available capacity by relevant market/window; return separate qualification and acquisition hypotheses. |
| PK06-E2 | B05, B07, E03 | Separate total transaction value, own consideration, seller funds, reserves, chargebacks and settlement responsibilities using current reviewed definitions. |
| PK06-E3 | G06, G08, E02 | Measure eligible requests successfully matched and eligible supply used on stated horizons, including failures and time-to-match; do not infer network effects from registrations or total GMV. |

All effects preserve the shared evidence, rights, calculation, mutation and growth invariants; they add no external authority.

| Dimension | Original dimension | Candidate treatment |
|---|---|---|
| D01 | customer / buyer structure | Buyer, supplier, intermediary, payer and beneficiary roles are explicit on each side. |
| D02 | offer grammar | Matching/service promise and counterparty duties are separated; no guaranteed liquidity. |
| D03 | pricing unit | Transaction fee, subscription, lead fee or justified combination with a clear payer. |
| D04 | money model | Transaction value differs from own fees; each additional exchange has costs and obligations. |
| D05 | channel priorities | Acquire the side and market implicated by evidence, not both sides indiscriminately. |
| D06 | sales path | Qualification, search/request, match, agreement, completion and dispute remain separate. |
| D07 | delivery model | Matching and counterparty delivery depend on actual eligible supply and trust/recovery work. |
| D08 | retention model | Repeat value and leakage are side-specific; listings and paid renewals have different meanings. |
| D09 | unit economics | Own contribution includes support, incentives, disputes and payment exposure; not GMV margin. |
| D10 | capacity constraints | Local/category/time supply, demand and moderation/support constrain transaction commitments. |
| D11 | cash behaviour | Third-party funds and reserves are not available growth cash; settlement/refund dates matter. |
| D12 | core metrics | Matched eligible requests / eligible requests; supplied participation and time-to-match separately. |
| D13 | experiment types | Bounded manual match or market-cell test with genuine participants and explicit authority. |
| D14 | quality criteria | Match relevance, completion, counterparty quality and rights; no fake listings or social proof. |

**Discriminating design case S06:** Four matches from ten eligible requests is 40%, not 250%. Keep unmet demand and third-party funds visible; a high transaction value does not establish platform revenue or permission to subsidise growth. Exact inputs/prompts and the actual bounded local comparisons are in the [design probes](research-logs/2026-09-10-stage-14-design-probes.md). They do not establish installed-pack performance.

### PK09 — `local-service-business`

**Design disposition:** RETAIN AS DESIGN CANDIDATE. **Implementation status:** NOT IMPLEMENTED.

**Research basis:** R16, R04 in the research companion.
