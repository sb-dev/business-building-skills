## 1. Evaluation protocol


Separate three questions. First, did the run actually execute with the right installation, inputs and authority? Second, are its numbers and record identities correct? Third, does the actual output reason adequately about the case? A structurally complete persuasive document can fail the third question. A responsible business decision to stop or seek evidence can pass response conformance.

For every relevant reasoning/behaviour dimension, record PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED or NOT APPLICABLE with an actual output/evidence locator and explanation. PASS means the bounded response meets that dimension's contract; it does not mean the business should grow. FAIL identifies a violated requirement. BLOCKED identifies unavailable evaluation evidence or a dependency that prevents assessing the response. NOT APPLICABLE requires a specific scope justification and cannot conceal a relevant unknown. Preserve the business's own GG01–GG07 and interpretation/next-decision findings separately. If the business correctly reports missing cash evidence, the response can PASS appropriate uncertainty handling while its cash-dependent commercial commitment remains BLOCKED.

Do not average the sixteen business dimensions into a startup or opportunity score. A case-conformance gate can require all mandatory obligations without turning it into a single measure of business quality. Fabricated evidence, misleading terms, overridden rights, unauthorised effects, known unfunded/overloaded scale approval, or an ungrounded broad rewrite are critical failures. Good prose, other dimensions and conversion estimates cannot offset them.

The grader receives the frozen subject input, actual response/tool traces and separate oracle. It cites specific claims and supporting/contradicting facts, checks calculation evidence, recognises permitted alternatives, and considers whether a failure is local or disqualifies the requested commitment. Evaluate meaning; do not use required-word counts, output length, similarity to a reference paragraph or keyword spotting as semantic proof. Business tasks admit multiple adequate strategies and legitimate inconclusive outcomes. Adjudicate genuine disagreement against the exact facts and requirement, recording both assessments; do not secretly move the goalposts. An LLM grader is an evaluation aid with recorded identity/configuration, not independent professional authority.

Use opaque condition labels when feasible for paired assessment and do not reveal which arm should win. Keep expected findings and authored adequate candidates outside subject prompts. All mandatory cases and failed/aborted attempts remain in the record. A retry is a separate attempt with a reason, never a replacement that erases the first result. If making a broader reliability or superiority claim, predefine a justified repeat/sample/analysis plan; one paired case only establishes the observed case-specific difference. No unmeasured token saving, economic improvement or success rate is inferred.

Use a fresh subject context and authorised output directory for each attempt. Freeze prompt, fixed inputs, installed revision, pack condition, permitted tools and rubric before assessment. Capture the full actual output and tool/error trace, then grade it separately with the hidden oracle. Preserve incomplete attempts and explain retries. No oracle or authored adequate control may leak into the subject input. Correct alternative decisions and correctly blocked commercial proposals can pass conformance.

Pack authoring acceptance also follows Stage 14's eleven-step workflow and P01–P07: inspect the real catalogue, evidence the insufficiency, research the mechanism, define effects/economics, build a full showcase and exact prompt, add positive/negative criteria, execute both arms for an implemented claim, validate and catalogue honestly. Test create and revise paths, including a no-effect pack that must be rejected and a revision that must preserve unaffected facts/rights. A stage-17 design record cannot satisfy later authored-pack execution or measured-comparison requirements.

## 9. Regression policy

### 5. Complete regression loop

| ID / original requirement | Evaluation method | Adequate anchor | Failing anchor | Case witnesses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RG01 — escaped business-reasoning defect | Capture the actual reported output/tool trace, exact prompt/data, host/core/pack version, settings and failing claim. | A genuine escape is provenance-linked; authored seeds are labelled synthetic and kept distinct. | Invent an old agent failure or treat a suggested future issue as an escaped defect. | E10, AD09 |

| RG03 — create smallest reproducible case | Reduce irrelevant context while preserving the failed relationship, protected versions and exact failing witness. | The small case still exhibits the defect and the full parent case remains registered. | Delete the difficult condition, shrink mandatory primary coverage or leak the answer key to the subject. | E10, AD09 |

#### Preservation, repair and worked rehearsal

Retain every accepted source version before the review. An evaluator writes an assessment; it never silently edits its subject. Compare no change, a local repair, a bounded evidence test and any proposed coupled repair. Require a fact-based explanation for every changed layer and a disposition for each genuinely affected dependency. Correct arithmetic does not make a broad customer/offer/price/delivery/brand rewrite proportionate.

The AD09 fixture is a small synthetic rehearsal of the acquisition-quality failure family also covered by accepted E02/E10. Its authored old answer selects channel A from cost per lead, despite CAC 100 and after-acquisition contribution -80, while B has CAC 20 and residual 50. The corrected authored answer recognises those differences without asserting causal superiority or safe scale. The separate calibration record applies R08/R09/R13 to both texts and records why the old text fails and the repaired text passes this bounded claim review. This rehearses evaluator discrimination; it is not an escaped installed-agent defect or a repaired installed-skill run.

When a real escape occurs, RG01–RG07 require actual captured/replayed old and repaired evidence. Preserve the full E02/E10 parent cases alongside the minimal regression, rerun the affected calculation, channel, qualification, economics and preservation checks, and widen downstream review only where the changed layer has real dependencies. If the defect is a calculation or grader error, repair that owner instead of rewriting business facts or skill policy. Keep the old evidence and the new version permanently; release/maturity gates remain closed while mandatory failures or blocked evaluation evidence remain unresolved.

## 10. Installation and release gates

Specification 03 defines the actual source, selective and clean consumer procedures. Test each installation condition separately on its real host: command discovery, standalone entry and local resources, prerequisites, modes, fitting/non-fitting activation, calculation and accepted-input preservation. The clean consumer must have no source checkout or siblings available to supply undeclared files. Exercise packaged examples using complete input resources; an installation script or directory listing does not establish this result.

| Gate | Required passing evidence | Failure/block consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Benchmark design completeness | Every original bootstrap §24 concern, complete case contract, all primaries/stress cases, adversarial controls, pack requirements and regression steps; actual design checks and substantive review | Repair current design; no completion from file presence or intent. |
| Run integrity | Actual allowed installation/configuration, exact input/prompt/condition, preserved source and complete raw output/tool/error evidence | Missing/contaminated evidence invalidates or blocks the relevant execution claim; retain the attempt. |
| Deterministic correctness | Relevant D01–D10 checks and actual arithmetic/input/output identities | Known incorrect numbers/definitions fail their claim; unavailable material tools/inputs block assessment. |
| Semantic and behavioural conformance | Relevant R01–R16 and BH01–BH10 findings with evidence; mandatory invariants satisfied; justified decision and repair scope | Critical failures cannot be offset by prose quality, other dimensions or commercial optimism. |
| Progressive and stress coverage | All 15 primary examples at accepted depth plus complete A/B/C and adversarial coverage for the claimed product state | A subset is labelled scoped development evidence and cannot pass full coverage. |
| Implemented pack claims | Each implemented pack meets all seven requirements, with actual paired core/pack outputs and authoring create/revise evidence | A design projection, missing arm or catalogue entry cannot establish implementation or measured behaviour. |
| Installation/product integrity | Source and distinct clean external/selective installation actually executed; required local resources and documented prerequisites resolve | Repository-only tests cannot clear consumer installation. |
| Regression and promotion | Actual old defect rejected, actual repair accepted, affected cases rerun and permanent history retained; all mandatory gates for the claimed maturity pass | Keep maturity/public claims limited; no release/registry/PR readiness from incomplete or fabricated evidence. |

A full product claim requires all fifteen primary cases, all canonical stress and adversarial requirements, all seven obligations for each implemented pack, pack-author create/revise and rejection cases, applicable permanent regressions, deterministic correctness, semantic/behaviour conformance and distinct actual installation evidence at compatible revisions. Exact counts cannot be replaced by a development subset. Report all required/actual counts and every mandatory failure or block.

Release evidence must identify revision, distribution manifest, commands actually run, host/settings, input/output hashes, tool/calculation results, graders, dimensions, deviations and unresolved limitations. A conditional test environment supports only that environment. An actual compatible paired run supports only the observed case-specific difference unless a justified repeated evaluation supports a broader claim. No benchmark result is customer validation or professional endorsement. Passing technical gates grants no release, merge, registry or live business authority.

Historical Stage 16 and 17 verifiers establish design/calibration checks only. Preserve their actual receipts without rerunning them over mutated progress inputs and relabelling the results. New production verification has its own entry points and evidence. The end-to-end audit after Stage 26 remains mandatory before PR readiness.
