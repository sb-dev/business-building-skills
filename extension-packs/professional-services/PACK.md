# Professional Services

## Thesis

Specialise Business Building for human-delivered project/retainer services where scope, sales effort, delivery capacity, non-billable work and collection timing are first-class constraints.

## Activate when

The primary exchange is a client paying for defined human expertise/work, including fixed-scope projects or retainers.

## Do not activate when

The primary exchange is a self-serve downloadable product, pure software subscription, marketplace transaction or goods sale merely because the founder is a consultant.

## Core effects

- `model-customer-value`: distinguish client/buyer/user and stakeholders receiving the work.
- `design-offer`: make scope, exclusions, client dependencies, change control and acceptance explicit.
- `design-pricing-monetisation`: compare project/retainer/day-rate only where relevant; no recurring default.
- `model-delivery-economics`: include delivery hours, non-billable sales/admin/support basis, capacity and effective contribution.
- `design-sales-path`: default hypothesis is sales-assisted/consultative only when evidence fits.
- `scale-channel`: delivery headroom and client concentration constrain acquisition.

## Economics and cash

Track project/period revenue, attributable delivery/subcontractor costs, non-billable effort separately, payment milestones/receivables, available hours and client concentration. Do not assume 100% utilisation or sell the same reserved capacity twice.

## Common experiments

Offer/price conversation, paid diagnostic, bounded project pilot, referral/channel test, project-vs-retainer comparison where continuing value exists.

## Professional surfaces

Client contracts, confidentiality/IP, contractor/employment classification and tax may require specialist review. The pack does not decide them.

## Showcase exact prompt

> Build a complete one-person Forward Deployment Engineer consultancy model from the supplied experience, target-client evidence and weekly capacity. Define customer/buyer, problem, offer, project/retainer pricing, acquisition and consultative sales path, delivery capacity, unit economics, cash timing, assumptions and first three experiments. Optimise for a sustainable solo business, not maximum lead volume.

## Differential expectations

Core+pack makes human capacity, non-billable effort, scope/change control, collection timing and concentration first-class. It must not invent SaaS retention, self-serve conversion or recurring revenue.
