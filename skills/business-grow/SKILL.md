# Business Grow

Grow a defined business system without treating reach, contacts or attributed revenue as sufficient evidence.

## Rules

Preserve accepted customer/offer facts unless evidence implicates them. Define funnel units/windows. Separate contact, prospect, qualified opportunity, customer and delivered value. Check permission, sales effort, delivery capacity, contribution and cash before scale. Never infer external-write authority from tool access.

## Commands

`select-channel`, `design-acquisition-test`, `design-sales-path`, `diagnose-conversion`, `design-retention-expansion`, `scale-channel`.

This is a Stage 21 scaffold; command/reference implementation follows in later stages.
