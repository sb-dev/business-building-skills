# design-pricing-monetisation

Input: offer, customer evidence, alternatives, costs/capacity and candidate transaction structures.

Output: a small set of price/monetisation hypotheses with payer, pricing unit, payment timing, economic implications, downside and evidence needed.

Acceptance: no universal premium-price rule; stated willingness to pay is distinguished from realised purchase; recurring payment requires continuing value hypothesis.

Forbidden: fictitious anchors, mandatory offer ladder, hidden renewal/fees.
