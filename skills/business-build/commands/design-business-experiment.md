# design-business-experiment

Input: material assumption, current evidence, decision, authority and cost/risk constraints.

Output: falsifiable experiment contract from `references/contracts.md`.

Acceptance: success/failure/inconclusive can lead to different decisions; representation is adequate to the claim; instrumentation/data-quality checks are included where relevant.

Forbidden: deceptive fake product, post-hoc success rule, universal sample size, customer contact without authority.
