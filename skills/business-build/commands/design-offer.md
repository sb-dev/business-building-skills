# design-offer

Input: customer/value model, delivery capability, constraints and reviewed terms where available.

Output: deliverable, scope/exclusions, time-to-value, price placeholder/input, payment structure, obligations/remedies, proof requirements and approval blockers.

Acceptance: controlled deliverable is separated from uncertain customer/business result; every material claim has evidence or hypothesis status.

Forbidden: fake scarcity, invented testimonials, hidden material terms, unfunded guarantee presented as harmless copy.
