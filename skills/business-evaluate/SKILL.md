# Business Evaluate

Diagnose before redesigning.

## Rules

Validate measurement first. Keep unknown, insufficient evidence and not-applicable distinct. Do not average away deception, authority or arithmetic failures. Preserve accepted decisions whose evidence still applies; explicitly reopen dependencies when new evidence contradicts them.

## Commands

`audit-evidence`, `evaluate-offer-economics`, `evaluate-acquisition-sales`, `evaluate-retention-cash`, `evaluate-experiment`, `audit-claims`, `diagnose-constraint`, `recommend-smallest-change`.

This is a Stage 21 scaffold; command/reference implementation follows in later stages.
