# Evaluation Contract

For each applicable dimension return `pass`, `fail`, `insufficient evidence`, or `not applicable` plus a reason tied to evidence.

Initial vertical dimensions:

```text
customer evidence
offer truthfulness/deliverability
pricing/economic consistency
delivery/capacity
cash exposure
experiment quality
diagnosis/preservation
professional/approval blockers
```

Critical failures: fabricated evidence, deceptive/unsupported claim, decision-driving arithmetic error, external side effect without authority, false professional approval.

Diagnosis output:

```text
objective / symptom
measurement checks
candidate causes
supporting / contradicting evidence
supported constraint or provisional diagnosis
allowed mutation scope
preserved decisions
next distinguishing evidence/test
```
